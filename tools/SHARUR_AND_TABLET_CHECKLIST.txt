SHARUR + THE TABLET OF DESTINIES - CHECKLIST (Nov 2026)
=======================================================
Two new collectibles, both on the Chamber of Destiny's west plinths. Item work
follows tools/ADDING_AN_ITEM.txt. [x] = done, [ ] = to do.

DESIGN (from the brief + answers)
  - TABLET OF DESTINIES: a flag item, Underworld only. Texture "tablet of
    destinies.png". Description: "Holds the irrevocable decrees of fate. Its
    owner has supreme authority."
  - SHARUR: a new WEAPON (texture sharur.png for its icons; the held model is
    assets/props/Sharur.smx -> SHARURWP.SMD). In the menu's weapon column, and
    the BOTTOM box of the weapon wheel.
      * The Crucifaxe's swing, twice as fast (swing AND cooldown).
      * Double damage to every enemy (2 per connected swing, not 1).
      * Charge: a tap swings at once, like the Crucifaxe. Keep holding after
        the swing and it slowly glows, peak at 3 s. Release at full charge:
        it swings and fires a large ')'-shaped blade of light forward, fast,
        until it hits a wall. 4 damage + knockback to every enemy it passes
        through; it ALSO breaks crates, breakable doors and vine curtains.
        Release before full charge: nothing.
  - PLACEMENT: Chamber of Destiny, the west plinths. Sharur on the centre of
    the LOWER plinth, the Tablet on the centre of the HIGHER plinth.
    Not collected by walking up. Each plinth has "Press O" in front of it.
    Circle: the camera is fixed; after 1 s the item rises a little, glows
    2 s, then moves toward the camera; the pickup sound plays and it goes
    into the inventory.
