DESIGN (from the brief)
  - The slab: assets/props/Lightning Tablet.smx, exported in place in
    Lightnings Edge: 1200 long x 700 tall x 100 thick, x[-600,600]
    y[-700,0] z[2450,2550]. The FRONT is the z=2450 face (faces -Z, the
    reader looks +Z, yaw 0 - the Wings Tablet's orientation).
  - Face layout (texture "lightning tablet.png"): a cloud frieze along the
    top (y -700..-600), Ninurta on the right (x 200..600), and a 4 x 3 grid
    of 200 x 200 squares (x -600..200, y -600..0). Each front square is a
    fan of 4 triangles round its centre; each back square one quad.
  - Every room entry: all 12 squares dealt a random quarter turn, never the
    correct one.
  - "Press O to interact" sign; Circle flies the camera to a face-on shot.
    A cursor picks any of the 12 squares (d-pad); SQUARE turns the square
    under it 90 degrees clockwise (the Anzu Tablet's button - round 2); Cross leaves at any time (the turns stay
    as they are for the rest of the visit).
  - Only the 8 squares carrying YELLOW lightning have to be upright:
        row 0 (top): cols 0, 1, 3     row 1: cols 1, 2, 3     row 2: cols 1, 2
    The 4 blue-only squares never matter.
  - ONE AMBIGUITY in the art, measured: row 1 col 1's yellow enters at the
    top and leaves at the bottom at points that swap exactly under a 180
    degree turn, so upside down it LOOKS connected. Accepted at 0 or 180
    (snapped upright as the scene starts), and never dealt at 180.
  - Solved: a short scene in the same shot. The yellow lights up as a fuse
    from the cloud to the bolt in Ninurta's hand (the Seal's lines: additive
    halo + core + a spark on the front), then Ninurta glows briefly, then the
    slab shatters as the others do and leaves the LIGHTNING BOLT.
  - Lightning Bolt: a new collectible, texture "Lightning Bolt.png".
    Description: "The life force of the Gods, carved in stone."
  - Debug option: puzzle solved, Lightning Bolt in the inventory, slab gone.

ROUND 2 (the user's report, same day)
  [x] BUG: on entering the room the face showed the SOLVED art and the puzzle
      could never be solved. Cause: the deal (lightning_tablet_arm, from the
      room's init) wrote the random turns into lt_rot but skipped writing
      them into the mesh, because it was gated on lt_live(), which tests
      current_area - and main.c only sets current_area to the room AFTER the
      init (STATE_LOADING, main.c ~7435). So the art said "solved" while the
      puzzle believed every square was turned: straightening the picture put
      each square back on its random value. Not a memory problem. The first
      headless run missed it because the force boot pre-sets current_area.
      Fix: lt_loaded() (mesh read this visit, still in the arena) for the
      deal and apply_flags; lt_live() (also in the room) for per-frame paths.
  [x] Square turns the squares (was Circle); the hint reads "[] - Turn".
  [x] Re-verified headless WITHOUT pre-setting current_area (a real
      STATE_LOADING order).
