DESTINY'S DOOR - THE JEWEL CEREMONY - CHECKLIST (Nov 2026)
==========================================================
Hero's Plinth's frame (west wall) resolves the three mini-boss flags. Code in
src/heros_plinth.c; the spirit glow shared through src/spirit_glow.c.
[x] = done, [ ] = to do.

DESIGN (from the brief)
  - Three jewels on the frame's face, each the vertex where four triangles
    meet (x=-1700): LEFT (z=-600, y=-539) YELLOW, TOP (z=0, y=-970) GREEN,
    RIGHT (z=+600, y=-539) BLUE. Left/right are as the player sees them
    facing the door (west), so left is -z - the same sense as the frame's
    "bottom left"/"bottom right" textures.
  - Spirits: ADAPA blue -> right jewel, HADAD green -> top, GULA yellow ->
    left.
  - "Press O to interact" at the frame's centre. Circle:
      * none of the three dead: the log says "A marble structure. It looks
        important." Nothing else; the prompt stays.
      * otherwise THE CEREMONY: the camera takes over, glides to the room's
        centre and looks up into the sky away from the door (east). The
        spirits of the DEAD bosses appear far off (the death scenes' glow,
        number for number) and fly in slowly; the camera follows the three
        (its path is the same whichever are dead). Each flies into its jewel:
        a full-screen flash of its colour, the spirit goes, the jewel's glow
        fades up from nothing. Adapa first, Hadad 1 s later, Gula 0.5 s
        after that. Then the whole frame in shot, 1 s, and - ONLY IF ALL
        THREE - Destiny's Door's texture fades up over the 30 recessed polys
        (x=-1750). Control back at the room's centre.
  - Afterwards (saved): the dead bosses' jewels keep glowing. All three: the
    door stays, and "Press O to enter" -> "COMING SOON" in the log. Fewer:
    no prompt at all, ever again.
  - Debug option DESTINY DOOR: all three dead and the ceremony done.
  - Then each death scene's rising spirit takes its colour here.
