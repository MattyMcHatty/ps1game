PS1 HORROR GAME — ASAG'S ARENA: WHAT IS RESERVED, AND WHAT IT COST
===================================================================
Asag is the game's SECOND boss.

>>> SEPTEMBER 2026, SECOND PASS: THE WHOLE BOSS WAS REPLACED. <<< Everything
the first version built — eight drawn parts, fourteen clips, seven textures and
the arena they stood in — was thrown away and rebuilt from new art. What exists
now is a new arena mesh, a new collision proxy, a ONE-PART animated body with
SIX clips — FIVE today; the emerge went in the third pass, see PART 8 — three
textures, and a DEMO director (src/asag_boss.c) that played them end to end on a
loop so they could be looked at.

>>> SEPTEMBER 2026, THIRD PASS: THE OPENING SCENE IS BUILT. <<< The demo
director is gone and src/asag_boss.c is a real encounter script for as long as
the scene lasts: the camera falls out of the air onto the landing and BOUNCES,
pans up onto Asag held two seconds into his faint, then drifts UP AND TO THE
RIGHT as the faint carries him back to Home until it is looking slightly down at
his face; he drops into a looping idle, his two boils light up over two seconds
and are left pulsing, the demon speech and the boss music start together over
two lines of subtitle, and then the camera returns to the landing and the player
has it back exactly where they arrived. The same pass replaced the
ARRIVING TRANSITION — the drop used to play a garden gate creaking open and now
plays four mud quads rushing the camera (DOOR_PANEL_FALL) — and filled the
arena's SPU bank, which was empty.

>>> SEPTEMBER 2026, SIXTH PASS: READABILITY, THE TWO SAFE SQUARES, AND A
CRASH ON DEATH. <<< Nothing new was
built; three things that were already there stopped lying about themselves.

  THE BOILS ARE HITTABLE FROM ANY ANGLE NOW. The proxy's back wall is at
  z=2700 and the boil faces are at z=2734, so every shot at a boil has to be
  cleared to a point SHORT of the wall standing inside it - and the backoff
  that did that was a flat 80 world units off the end of the shot. That is
  only correct square-on: off the axis the line covers the 34-unit gap in
  34/cos(theta) of its own length, which is past 80 by about 63 degrees. The
  Grave-olver never showed it (4000 of range means it is fired from down the
  arena, where the angle is shallow); the HELLUMINATOR did, because 1800 of
  range forces the player up to the back of the room where almost every
  position that is not directly in front of a boil is steeper than that. The
  symptom was "the lantern only burns a boil looking straight at it".
  asag_boil_clear_depth() (src/asag_fight.c) replaces the flat number with the
  exact fraction - backoff / (boil_z - cam_z) of the shot, which is the same
  answer at every heading - and both weapons call it.

  THE EXPOSURE WINDOW HAS A TELL. asag_exposed() is the whole of the fight's
  difficulty and there was nothing on screen that said when it was open; the
  player was expected to read it off which attack was playing, and each window
  closes AF_EXPOSE_LEAD before its clip does. The yellow spot on the crown of
  his head now glows BLUE while he is exposed - asag_set_head_glow(), driven
  from asag_fight.c beside the damage flash, drawn in asag.c. It is five
  primitives at 1.31x spill (it went in at 1.63x and was a wash of blue over
  the whole crown), and WHICH five is decided at load time from the mesh's own UVs
  (the one bright yellow disc in textures/asag.png at u[85,100] v[113,123])
  rather than from an index list, so a re-export cannot silently light some
  other part of him.

  >>> AND DYING IN HERE CRASHED THE GAME. <<< The report was "the game-over
  screen will not let me press Start to get back to the main menu, and it does
  in every other level". It let you press it. What it did next was jump to
  0xcf73fca0.

  THE GAME-OVER SCREEN AND reset_game() WERE BOTH INNOCENT, and proving that
  first is what found this: killing the player in the arena and taking the Start
  branch reaches "at title" with zero exceptions. The fault is on the NEXT line.

  Returning to the title does NOT go through STATE_LOADING, and STATE_LOADING is
  the only thing that calls asags_free_model(). Dying goes straight from the
  game-over screen to STATE_TITLE, so it arrived holding all 204 KB of the boss
  - and the first thing the return-to-title hook does is sound_bank_select(
  SND_BANK_INTRO), which mallocs a buffer per VAG and CdReads into it. Unlike
  src/asag.c's read_file(), load_vag_at() in src/sound.c HAS NO STACK GUARD. So
  the intro bank allocated up against the stack and DMA'd over the return
  address. PART 6A's crash, in a different room, three passes later.

  The fix is two idempotent calls in that hook (src/main.c), freeing both boss
  models BEFORE the bank swap - the order src/asag.h already insists on. The NEW
  GAME hook a few lines above was already doing exactly this, and says in its own
  comment that it has to because that path skips STATE_LOADING; the return-to-
  title hook is the other half of the same argument and had been missed. The
  Rabisu had the identical latent crash on dying in the Garden Courtyard.

  >>> IF A THIRD PATH OUT OF A BOSS ROOM IS EVER ADDED, IT OWES THE SAME TWO
  CALLS. <<< And load_vag_at() would be worth giving read_file()'s stack guard,
  which would turn this whole class of failure from a wild jump into a silent
  missing sound effect.

  >>> AND THE ARENA HAD TWO SQUARES THAT NOTHING COULD REACH. <<< Read the room
  as the 3x3 its thirds make it, numbered from Asag's end - 1 2 3 across his
  end, 4 5 6 the middle, 7 8 9 the landing. The slam hit 1-2-3, the laser
  7-8-9 and the vomit the centre column 2-5-8, so SQUARES 4 AND 6, the middle
  row's two flanks, were hit by nothing at all: a seat to fight the whole boss
  from. The three-zones note had said since the fourth pass to "change one zone
  and check it against the other two", and that instruction was obeyed and
  still missed this, because what matters is not how the zones overlap but what
  none of them covers. CHECK THE LEFTOVER.

  Two attacks grew into it, from the directions they were already coming from:

    SLAM   Asag's third PLUS 4 and 6 - an L opening toward the landing - with
           TWO MORE BOULDERS, at columns 2 and 12 (x -1000 / +1000) in row 7,
           which is the same line across the room his head lands on. Four
           boulders now, four stagger slots, last impact at t 184 of a 375-tick
           clip.
    VOMIT  the centre lane PLUS the whole middle row - a plus. Half the spray
           particles now throw ACROSS the crossbar instead of down the lane, so

  Nothing is safe from all three now (laser leaves 1-6, slam leaves 5/7/8/9,

  BOTH ZONES ARE SHAPES RATHER THAN BANDS NOW, so each is one predicate on

    THE OPENING SCENE IS TWO SHOTS INSTEAD OF ONE SLIDE. The third pass's scene

##########################################################################
 PART 1 — WHAT THE ARENA IS, AND WHY ITS SHAPE IS LOAD-BEARING
##########################################################################
ONE WAY IN: the 1200-unit drop down the pit in The Hatch's yard, after both
keyholes are turned. main.c takes that transition at hatch_puzzle_drop_done().

>>> AND, AS OF THIS PASS, NO WAY OUT. <<< The first arena had a placeholder exit
standing at the shaft mouth, offering to climb a shaft the fiction says cannot
be climbed, with main.c's destination for it gating back into The Hatch and
flagged as a placeholder in as many words. The new mesh has no door in it
either, and where the room actually leads has still not been decided — so rather
than ship the lie a second time, the exit is GONE: no prompt, no trigger, no
destination, and no asag_arena_exit_* API at all.

The only ways out of the room today are the DEBUG LEVEL SELECT (the "ASAG ARENA"
row, which is back in title.c for exactly this reason) and dying. A save made
BEFORE the drop is the only route back into the rest of the game. That is a
deliberate hole, it is written down in src/asag_arena.h, and it is the first
thing to fix when the fight is real.

>>> EVERY BUDGET BELOW FOLLOWS FROM THAT ONE-WAY SHAPE AND FROM NOTHING ELSE.
<<< Because nothing else in the game is drawn, heard or updated while the player
is down there, and because the player cannot arrive from anywhere but the shaft:

  - almost all of VRAM may be overwritten, and nothing has to be put back;
  - the SPU bank can be empty of shared effects;
  - the encounter can seal the door for its whole length and know that winning
    and dying are the only two exits, so there is no re-entry case and no
    "replay or skip the reveal" question.

Give the room a second door, a save point, or a way back up the shaft and every
one of those goes away. That is the one decision in this whole feature that is
expensive to revisit.

THE REAL GEOMETRY, as built: x[-1500,1500] z[0,2900], floor y=0, perimeter walls
to y=-1000, mesh detail up to y=-1534. The collision proxy is a perimeter with a
RECESS AT EACH END, both floored at y=0 and both narrower than the middle
(x[-1300,1300] against x[-1500,1500]):

    FLOOR 0   y=0   x[-1300,1300]  z[2613,2700]   the SOUTH recess, under Asag
    FLOOR 1   y=0   x[-1300,1300]  z[   0, 186]   the NORTH recess, the landing
    FLOOR 2   y=0   x[-1500,1500]  z[ 186,2613]   the arena floor itself

The player lands at x=0 z=300 facing +Z, straight down the arena at Asag.

>>> AND THE BOSS IS NOT IN THE ROOM. <<< Its bind pose spans z[2262,3931] —
a thousand units BEHIND the arena's own back wall — and y[-960,-528], so its
lowest point is 528 above the floor. Asag hangs over the arena out of the rock
behind it. Two consequences worth knowing before touching anything:

  - the cull distance is 7000 and NOT the room's own 5900 Manhattan diagonal,
    because a cull tight to the room would clip the far end of the boss off;
  - none of the boss's own collision walls can ever fire at rest (PART 7A).


##########################################################################
 PART 2 — VRAM: THREE TEXTURES, AND TWELVE FULL PAGES STILL FREE
##########################################################################
tools/VRAM_MAP_ASAG.txt is this room's map, generated by tools/vram_map_asag.py.
It is the third such map (after the whole-disc one and the garden-west bank's)
and by far the emptiest.

RESERVED — and it is exactly "what the player brought": the HUD panel, the
bullet-impact sprite, entity drop shadows; the Crucifaxe, the Grave-olver,
standard rounds, flame rounds, the Helluminator; the small medkit; every
collectible menu icon; and grdngtl + grdngtr, the GATE door panel.

>>> THE DOOR PANEL WAS THE SUBTLE ONE, AND IT IS NOW RESERVED AT ALL. <<<
door_anim draws its panel after the OUTGOING room's update and BEFORE
STATE_LOADING, so whichever panel a transition uses has to be in VRAM across it.
The arrival used to borrow DOOR_PANEL_GATE, which reserved grdngtl and grdngtr
in this bank — and meant a 1200-unit fall down a shaft was announced by a garden
gate creaking open.

The arrival is DOOR_PANEL_FALL now: four mud quads, flat in a 2x2 square, a long
way down the view axis, with the camera accelerating into them on t^2 and fading
to black before it arrives. Its texture is THIS ROOM'S OWN MUD, already owned at
x384 y0, so the bank reserves no panel any more.

>>> AND IT INVERTS THE USUAL RULE, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KNOWING. <<< Every
other panel in the game is in VRAM before its transition starts — put there once
at startup, or (the greenhouse door) guaranteed because both ends of the
transition upload it. Neither is true here: at the moment the fall begins, the
arena's uploader has not run and The Hatch's art is still at x384 y0. So
door_anim_start() streams the one TIM itself, through
asag_arena_upload_mud() — which owns the DrawSync(0) and the cdaudio bracket
that make a mid-game LoadImage legal (PROBLEM A in tools/TEXTURING_NOTES.txt).
It goes into the same rect and the same recorded tpage/clut the room will use,
which is why nothing new is reserved, and main.c calls cdaudio_stop() BEFORE
door_anim_start() rather than after so the bracket's resume cannot restart The
Hatch's music under the fall.

WHEN THE ROOM GROWS AN EXIT, whatever panel that uses comes back onto
RESERVED in tools/vram_map_asag.py.

RECLAIMABLE — everything else, and most of it is free rather than merely
available: every mansion and garden room re-uploads its own art on entry. THE
ARENA OWES NOBODY A RESTORE ON THE WAY OUT — trivially so today, since it has no
way out.

WHAT THIS BANK TAKES. THREE pages, all reclaimable, none owing a restore:

    mud        x384 y0    8bpp   the arena floor and its mud banks
    Boss Wall  x512 y0    8bpp   the perimeter
    asag       x640 y0    8bpp   THE BOSS'S SKIN — streamed by the ROOM

That leaves TWELVE full 8bpp pages plus the whole Voff-128 band, including
x320/x384 y128 — the shared spider/rafflesia sprite pair, 128 rows by 128
columns, the largest contiguous hole in the bank. main.c's STATE_LOADING branch
SKIPS that upload for this room specifically, so it is honestly free rather than
merely intended to be. It is the right shape for a boss sprite sheet.

For comparison: the Greenhouse, the most texture-hungry room in the game, owns
six pages. There is room here for twice that.

>>> THE BOSS'S SKIN IS IN THE ROOM'S TABLE, NOT REGISTERED BY THE BOSS. <<<
tools/ADDING_A_3D_ENEMY.txt STEP 3 says a textured enemy registers through
texmgr, which is right for the Rabisu and wrong here: that is ~17 KB of
PERMANENT main RAM for art that can only be seen behind a one-way drop, and this
room already makes a bracketed CD read on the only transition that reaches it.
Zero permanent bytes instead. src/asag.c reads the tpage/clut back through
asag_arena_tex_page()/asag_arena_tex_clut().

>>> ALL THREE TEXTURES ARE 128x128, AND THAT IS THE SMX'S DECISION, NOT THE
ENGINE'S. <<< The source art in textures/asag/ is three different sizes (asag
128, Boss wall 256, mud 64). io_export_smx_v3.py normalises every UV in an
export to exp_tex_size=128 regardless of the image it came from, and the arena's
own UVs prove it — mud runs u[10..147] and Boss Wall u[7..117], both addressed
over a 128-texel tile. Ship mud at its native 64 and UV 127 reads off its right
edge into whatever sits next in VRAM; ship Boss Wall at 256 and only its
top-left quarter is ever sampled. So the three are RESAMPLED to 128x128 on the
way from textures/asag/*.png to textures/*.png, and png_to_tim.py runs on those.

  Going genuinely bigger is possible and is not worth it: 256 forces 4bpp (16
  colours), a whole VRAM tpage across BOTH Voff halves, and a per-poly DR_TWIN
  bracket. tools/TEXTURING_NOTES.txt PART 5 has the full cost, and records that
  the one 256x256 attempt in this game — the Greenhouse's cuniform pipes — was
  reverted after 16 of its 40 faces straddled a tile edge and lost their art.

>>> AND MIND THE CLUT BAND. <<< The first pass put three CLUTs at x512 in the
y>=480 band, where they landed inside ddog_alert.tim's PIXELS — the exact trap
ADDING_A_3D_ENEMY.txt STEP 3 warns about. Do not reason about that band by hand:
place the TIM, regenerate tools/VRAM_MAP.txt and read its CLUT OVERLAP CHECK.
The three live at 672,499 / 672,500 / 672,501.

ADDING ONE: put the .tim in textures/, add a <file> to disc.xml's TEXASAG dir
(NOT the TEX dir — see PART 4A), add a row to stream_tex_file[] in
src/asag_arena.c, a row to ASAG_TEX_* in src/asag_arena.h, a row to BANK in
tools/vram_map_asag.py and, if an arena polygon uses it, a row to NAME_TO_SLOT
in gen_asag_arena_tex_map.py. Then regenerate BOTH maps. No generator run for
tim_slots.h — this room captures tpage/clut from the TIM at read time precisely
so a texture can be added without one.


##########################################################################
 PART 3 — SPU: THE WHOLE 232 KB REGION, AND HOW IT WAS PAID FOR
##########################################################################
SND_BANK_ASAG (bit 16, src/sound.h) exists and holds ONE CLIP. main.c's
STATE_LOADING selects it for STATE_ASAG_ARENA and for nothing else.

>>> THE ONE CLIP IS SFX_DMNSPEAK, AND IT IS TAGGED BOSS|ASAG. <<< The opening
scene puts one line of the demon speech over each of its two subtitles, exactly
as the Rabisu's reveal does, and the two bosses sit in different banks — so the
clip carries a copy in each, the same two-copies answer SFX_GATE and SFX_RUMBLE
already take. It cost nothing anybody will notice:

                    bank_base   region    house    boss   garden   intro   asag   spare
    before          0x46150     237232   162560  190336  177216   22080      0    46896
    after           0x46150     237232   162560  190336  177216   22080  54016    46896

`spare` is the region less the LARGEST bank, and BOSS still sets it. So filling
this bank stays free until it becomes the largest, which for Asag means another
136 KB of clips.

AND IT DID NOT MOVE THE ARENA DOOR'S HEAP PEAK EITHER, which is the constraint
that actually binds down there (PART 6). sound_bank_select's read is a
55,296-byte malloc that is FREED before main.c loads the boss model, and main.c
sequences the two apart on purpose — so the peak is still the 108,544-byte model
read rather than the sum. Re-do that arithmetic when the fight's own clips land;
PART 6's warning below was written for exactly this and it has now been paid
once without cost, which is not a guarantee about the next one.

An almost-empty bank is still the point. The arena can hold no monster, so not
one shared effect has to be carried, and everything the PLAYER makes a noise
with is RESIDENT and unaffected by which bank is in:

    SWING  AXEHIT  GR_SHOT  GR_RELOAD  HURT  DIE  STEP1  STEP2
    PICKUP  SMASH  SLAM  UNLOCK  DOOR  RBS_SWING  CURSOR  SELECT  BACK

That list is already complete. Nothing needs adding to the bank to make the
player work; every byte of it is Asag's.

>>> THE REGION WAS GROWN BY 47 KB TO GET THERE, AND IT IS WORTH KNOWING HOW. <<<
The Rabisu's FIREBALL, BOOM and EXPLODE were SND_RESIDENT — 47,808 bytes — on
the reasoning that they fire mid-fight and a bank swap is a CD read. That
reasoning was sound and the conclusion was wrong: all three are played by
src/rabisu.c and src/rabisu_boss.c alone, world.c places a Rabisu in the Garden
Courtyard alone, and SND_BANK_BOSS is loaded for the whole of that room. The
bank was already making the guarantee the residency was paying for. And a
resident clip is charged TWICE — once against the permanent block, and again
against the region, which is whatever is left after it.

They are SND_BANK_BOSS now. Before and after, from STEP 3 of
tools/ADDING_A_SOUND.txt:

                    bank_base   region    house    boss   garden   intro   asag   spare
    before          0x51C10     189424   162560  142528  177216   22080     -    12208
    after           0x46150     237232   162560  190336  177216   22080     0    46896

So every bank in the game gained 47 KB of ceiling and `spare` nearly quadrupled.
GARDEN is still the largest bank and still the one that sets `spare`.

>>> DO NOT REPEAT THE MISTAKE WITH ASAG. <<< Its arena is a one-way pocket whose
bank is loaded for the entire room, cutscene and fight alike, so an effect that
plays mid-fight belongs in SND_BANK_ASAG and NOT in the residents. A resident
clip would cost the whole game twice over for a room it can only ever be heard
in. Re-run the STEP 3 arithmetic with an `asag` list before writing any code —
an overflowing bank is SILENT, not broken.

Voices: 0..23, and Asag inherits a crowded map (sfx_channel in src/sound.c).
Voices 19, 20, 21 and 22 are EMERGE, DMNSPEAK, EXPLODE and BOOM — all BOSS-bank
now, so all four are guaranteed idle in the arena and are the natural borrowings
for Asag's long clips. 13, 14 and 15 (GAS, PULL, HISS) are GARDEN-bank and
likewise idle here.

>>> CDAUDIO_ASAG_TRACK POINTS AT TRACK 6 AND IS BORROWING IT. <<< The opening
scene needs music on its first subtitle and there is no master for this fight on
the disc, so the name currently resolves to the GARDEN COURTYARD'S track

GIVING ASAG HIS OWN IS TWO EDITS: one <track type="audio"> line APPENDED at the
foot of disc.xml, 44100 Hz 16-bit STEREO, and change the 6 in src/cdaudio.h back
to 9. Slot 9 is still reserved for it. Nothing else: no call site mentions a

room in the game plays the wrong music.

mkpsxiso accepts anything and silently converts, and what comes out of a mono
source is upsampled mono; music/mp3_to_wav.py is NOT the tool (it is the
sound-effect path and always mixes to mono). Convert with ffmpeg or pydub
straight to 44100 stereo. The one file in music/ not already on the disc is
elijah_k-unsolved-crime-473168.mp3, which has no .wav yet.

>>> IT IS THE BOSS'S TRACK, NOT THE ROOM'S. <<< main.c's STATE_LOADING branch
calls cdaudio_stop() for this room, so the arena is silent before the encounter
and after it, and the phase machine starts the track at the beat the brief
names. Do not replace that stop with nothing: a title-screen load or a debug
jump into the arena does not pass through the drop's own stop.


##########################################################################
 PART 4A — THE DISC DIRECTORY, WHICH IS NOT WHERE YOU EXPECT
##########################################################################
ASAG'S TEN FILES ARE IN \TEXASAG\, NOT \TEX\, and both halves of that name are
load-bearing.

WHY NOT \TEX\: its ISO9660 record already spans FIVE 2048-byte sectors, and the
note at the head of src/chain_room.c records a boot crash caused by growing it by
TWO files — the game died inside sound_init, in the first CdSearchFile that
switched from \TEX\ to \SND\, with 200 KB of heap spare and a clean VRAM map. A
directory of its own costs ONE record in the root, starts again at sector 1, and
leaves room for the fight's sounds and clips. The second pass took it from 32
records to 13, i.e. 764 of 2048 bytes used — there is room for the fight.

WHY "TEXASAG" AND NOT "ASAG": >>> mkpsxiso SORTS THE ROOT. <<< It writes root
records in identifier order, not in disc.xml order, and SYSTEM.CNF is the LAST
record in the root's first sector with 36 bytes to spare. A directory called
ASAG sorts BEFORE it and pushes it past byte 2048, at which point the boot ROM
cannot find it. tools/check_disc_root.py said so immediately:

    SYSTEM.CNF;1   record [2048,2108)  *** PAST 2048: WILL NOT BOOT ***

TEXASAG sorts after SYSTEM.CNF (and right after TEX), so its record lands in the
root's second sector where 1.5 KB is free. RUN check_disc_root.py AFTER ANY
CHANGE to disc.xml.

 PART 6 — MAIN RAM: THE CONSTRAINT THAT DECIDES EVERYTHING
##########################################################################
IT IS NOT VRAM AND IT IS NOT THE SPU. Read tools/HEAP_BUDGET.txt before doing
anything that allocates.

    FREE AT REST    174,116 bytes (170 KB)
    MEASURED FREE AT THE ARENA'S OWN DOOR   ~101 KB contiguous under the
                                            stack, ~93 KB once read_file's
                                            8 KB margin comes off

The room's own textures cost NOTHING — they stream on entry into a scratch that
is freed — and it registers nothing with texmgr. That was deliberate: the first
pass's six registrations would have been ~110 KB and would not have booted.

THE BOSS IS ROOM-SCOPED, WHICH IS WHAT MAKES ANY OF THIS FIT. The Rabisu used to
hold its model and its one clip from startup and never release them:

    RBSIDLE.PVA    75,776 bytes    ONE idle clip, 19 frames x 496 verts
    RABISU.SMD     28,672 bytes    the model
                  ---------
                  104,448 bytes    for a boss that exists in exactly one room

Both are now loaded from main.c's STATE_LOADING when the destination is the
Garden Courtyard and freed on every other transition — the same move
src/room_arena.c already made for room meshes. THE HEAP, not BSS, and that is
the whole point: unlike a room, NO boss is loaded almost all of the time, so a
BSS block would cost its full size in every room in the game.

>>> AND THE ORDER OF THOSE CALLS IS LOAD-BEARING. IT COST A CRASH TO FIND. <<<
Wiring the model up naively — one if/else, before the sound bank swap — made the
Garden Courtyard die on entry with the signature in
tools/DIAGNOSING_A_BOOT_CRASH.txt section 2. That door is the TIGHTEST MOMENT IN
THE GAME: it is the only transition that loads SND_BANK_BOSS, whose EMERGE clip
is a 71,680-byte malloc, and the 104,448-byte model was being loaded alongside
it. So main.c SEQUENCES them, and the free and the load are deliberately NOT one
if/else:

    /* before the bank swap */
    if (pending_area != STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_free_model();
    if (pending_area != STATE_ASAG_ARENA)       asags_free_model();
    ...
    sound_bank_select(...);
    /* after it */
    if (pending_area == STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_load_model();
    if (pending_area == STATE_ASAG_ARENA)       asags_load_model();

Free early so the outgoing model is gone before the incoming bank is read; load
late so the bank's buffer is already freed.

##########################################################################
 PART 6A — >>> heap_budget.py WAS WRONG BY 145 KB, AND IT COST A BOSS <<<
##########################################################################
READ THIS BEFORE BELIEVING ANY PRE-SEPTEMBER FIGURE ANYWHERE IN THE TREE.

Every "371 KB free" and "363 KB free" in the first pass's documents was produced
by a script that computed the heap as everything from _end to 0x801FFFF8 and
subtracted only texmgr and the kept buffers. THE TOP 145 KB OF THAT SPAN IS NOT
HEAP. src/main.c declares

    RenderContext ctx;

as a LOCAL in main(), and a RenderContext is two RenderBuffers — each an
OT_LENGTH(2048)-entry uint32 table plus a BUFFER_LENGTH(65536) packet buffer —
so main()'s stack frame is ~148 KB and it sits at the top of RAM for the entire
run. The heap can only ever grow to just underneath it.

WHAT IT COST. The first Asag was sized against the phantom figure: 229 KB of
meshes and clips at half rate, which "fit" 361 KB with 110 KB to spare. The
console crashed on EVERY entry to the arena, by the drop and by the debug menu
alike. Headless, with the model reads instrumented:

    ASGHLAS.PVA -> buffer 0x801D46D0 .. 0x801DE6D0
    sp = 0x801DBD40                      <- the stack pointer is INSIDE it
    Attempted unaligned JR to 0xfe51ffbd from 0x8008D9A0
    -> CdReadSync, cdread.c:160

That is precisely the mechanism tools/HEAP_BUDGET.txt and section 2 of
tools/DIAGNOSING_A_BOOT_CRASH.txt describe: malloc does not fail when the heap
fills, it hands back memory the stack is using and the next CdRead DMAs through
a return address.

>>> AND IT EXPLAINS THE "~234 KB CLIFF" THAT NEVER MADE SENSE. <<< That figure
was measured in the old units. 234 KB reported was about 86 KB real, which is
why a heap with "226 KB free" would not boot. The cliff was never mysterious;
the ruler was wrong.

WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT:
  - tools/heap_budget.py now subtracts a MEASURED stack floor (STACK_FLOOR, read
    as $sp inside read_file on this very transition) and prints the reserve as
    its own line. Re-measure it the same way if main()'s frame ever changes.
  - src/asag.c's read_file() reads $sp and REFUSES any allocation that would
    reach within 8 KB of it. A refused read leaves the mesh or a clip absent —
    a bind pose, or nothing drawn — which is visible and harmless. It can no
    longer corrupt the stack.
  - the clips are baked down (step 3 in this pass; the first pass needed 4).
  - the model load moved after the texture stream, so their peaks do not add.

>>> THE STACK RESERVE IS NOT RECOVERABLE BY MOVING ctx TO BSS. <<< It would move
the same 145 KB from the top of RAM to the bottom and shrink the heap by exactly
as much. What it WOULD do is make the accounting self-evident instead of
depending on a measured constant. Worth doing; not a win.

 PART 9 - THE FIGHT, AS BUILT

src/asag_fight.h is the specification and src/asag_fight.c is the machine. What
follows is the part that is NOT in either: the decisions, the measurements, and
the three things that were wrong first.

---- THE THREE FILES, AND WHY THERE ARE THREE ----------------------------
tools/ADDING_A_BOSS_ENCOUNTER.txt STEP 1 splits a boss in two: the BODY (model,
animation, damage, collision, combat AI) and the DIRECTOR (camera, lights,
music, seal, death). This pass kept that split and put the two halves of the
BODY in two translation units:

    src/asag.c        the mesh, the clips, the position track, the collider and
                      the geometry accessors. Decides nothing, as its header
                      has always said in capitals.
    src/asag_fight.c  the combat AI. NEW.
    src/asag_boss.c   the director. Gained the death sequence; the opening
                      scene was not touched.

>>> THE DEVIATION IS DELIBERATE AND IT IS ONE FILE, NOT A PATTERN. <<< The
runbook would have put the combat AI in src/asag.c. That file is 900 lines of
model and animation whose header opens by promising it is not the fight, and
the promise turned out to be worth keeping: what asag.c actually gained this
pass was four things, every one of them a question about the POSE -
asag_head_box(), asag_set_shake/fade(), asag_clip_ticks() and asag_ramp_home().
Not one of them knows there is a fight.

---- WHAT EVERY NUMBER WAS MEASURED FROM ---------------------------------
>>> ASAG CANNOT TURN, AND THAT ONE FACT SHAPES ALL THREE ATTACKS. <<< His
vertices are baked in world space and there is no model matrix (src/asag.h), so
a clip puts his head in exactly one place in the room every time it plays. An
attack lands where the ANIMATOR put it, and the only things code can aim are
the parts that LEAVE him.

Dumping the .pva files frame by frame WITH the position track applied - which
is the only way to see where the head really is - gives:

    LASER   settles t  75   at x  65  y -850  z 1923   held to t 280
    SLAM    settles t 112   at x -15  y  -66  z 1487   ON THE FLOOR
    VOMIT   settles t 112   at x -20  y -625  z 1657   twitches t 150..265
    FAINT   settles t 150   at x -10  y -170  z 1929   near the floor
    IDLE    Home            at x -14  y -780  z 2288

  (t is GAME FRAMES: frame * 60 / ASAG_ANIM_FPS. The position offset at t comes
   from update_pos() and clip_move[] in src/asag.c.)

Everything else follows:

  THE SLAM IS THE ONE ATTACK THE AXE CAN ANSWER, and that is geometry rather
  than design: the head is at y=-66 against a y=0 floor, in the middle of the
  arena, for the whole of an exposure window. src/crucifaxe.c got its first
  Asag block for exactly that window.

  THE LASER HAD TO SWEEP AND THE VOMIT HAD TO BE WIDE, because his head never
  moves off the line the animator put it on. What they sweep and how wide they
  are is PART 9A - the fifth pass replaced both.

---- THE BOILS, AND THE TABLE THAT HAD TO CHANGE -------------------------
asag_arena_boil[] used to be a FLAG - 1 for any of the eight faces - because the
opening scene lights all eight as one. The fight bursts them separately, so
gen_asag_arena_tex_map.py now emits a SIDE: 1 for the left cluster (x=-700) and
2 for the right (x=+900), off the sign of each face's own x. asag_arena.c holds
one level per boil and asag_arena_set_boil_glow() still sets both, which is why
the opening scene needed no edit at all.

>>> AND THE EIGHT INDICES ARE NOT CONTIGUOUS BY SIDE, WHICH IS THE BEST
ARGUMENT FOR THE DETECTION THERE HAS BEEN. <<< They come out

    440 441 442 443 444 445 446 447
     R   R   L   L   L   L   R   R

so the right-hand boil is 440,441,446,447. Anyone hand-writing "the first four
are one boil" would have been wrong, and the wrongness would have shown up as
half of each lump lighting.

---- THE COLLISION WALL THAT EATS EVERY SHOT AT A BOIL --------------------
>>> THIS IS THE RUNBOOK'S MISTAKE 2 IN A DIFFERENT HAT, AND IT WOULD HAVE BEEN
VERY HARD TO FIND. <<< The boil faces are at z=2734. The collision proxy's back
wall (wall 2 in src/asag_arena_mesh_collision.c) is at z=2700, spanning
x[-1300,1300] and the full height of the room - 34 units IN FRONT of the boils,
because the proxy is a rectangle and the lumps stand proud of the wall it
approximates.

So weapon_aim_clear() out to a boil's own depth reports EVERY shot blocked, by
a wall standing inside the target. The crosshair sits on a lit organ, the gun
fires, nothing happens, and nothing on screen says why.

The fix is ASAG_BOIL_CLEAR_BACKOFF (80 world units): clear the line to just
SHORT of the boil, which lands in front of z=2700 from anywhere in the room.
Both the gun and the lantern use it. The alternative - putting wall 2 in
shoot_over_mask - was rejected: it is a full-height perimeter wall and exempting
it would let every shot in the fight leave the room.

THE SAME WALL IS WHY THE PUSS BALLS DO NOT USE collision_segment_blocked().
src/web.c tests the segment just travelled so a fast shot cannot tunnel a thin
wall; here the launch point is BEHIND that wall and every ball would die on its
first frame. The arena is an empty box, so a bounds test is the whole of what
the segment test would have bought.

---- WHAT IT COST -------------------------------------------------------
NOTHING ON THE DISC. No new assets: the fight is code, and the boils were
already polygons of the arena mesh. disc.xml is untouched and
tools/check_disc_root.py still passes (root: 42 records, 460/2048 of the last
sector).

NOTHING IN VRAM. Everything the attacks draw is untextured: additive POLY_F4 for
the lights and the spray, flat POLY_F4 for the cubes.

NOTHING IN THE SPU. >>> NO SOUND PLAYS IN THE FIGHT OR IN THE DEATH. <<< That
was the brief for this pass - sound comes later - so the SPU arithmetic in
PART 3 has not been redone and the death's burn length is currently the
Rabisu's shape without the Rabisu's reason (RBE_T_D_BURN was the length of
SFX_EXPLODE). >>> WHEN THE CLIPS LAND, RE-DO PART 3's STEP 3 ARITHMETIC AND
RE-CHECK ABE_T_D_BURN AGAINST THE EXPLODE CLIP'S OWN LENGTH. <<<

MAIN RAM: 16,116 BYTES, AND THAT IS THE ONE FIGURE TO WATCH.

    FREE AT REST   before  192,332
    FREE AT REST   after   176,216

Nearly all of it is CODE, not state - the fight's own arrays are under a
kilobyte. af_cube() and af_glow_point() are inline GTE macros and those expand.

>>> WORK OUT WHAT THAT DOES TO THE ARENA'S DOOR BEFORE ADDING MORE. <<< PART 6
records 121-147 KB measured free at that door against a 100,352-byte model read.
That measurement was taken when FREE AT REST printed 172 KB - which is what it
prints again now - so the door is back where it was when it was pinned, not
past it. It is not slack. The next thing that costs 16 KB should be measured on
hardware at that door and not reasoned about, because the failure mode is
SILENT: read_file() declines the last clip in AsagClip order, which is the
FAINT, and a fight with no faint has no long exposure window and cannot be won.
Building src/asag_fight.c -Os gives 1,232 bytes back and was not taken; the
argument is in CMakeLists.txt beside the cold list.

---- THE THREE THINGS THAT WERE WRONG FIRST ------------------------------
1. THE PUSS BALLS' HEADING WAS SOLVED OVER A FULL TURN. The binary search that
   turns a direction into an angle is monotonic over a HALF turn and only over
   one; searching [-2048,2048] returns nonsense for half its inputs. It would
   have presented as balls occasionally thrown at the wrong wall, which is
   indistinguishable from "the spread is too wide". The fix takes the sign of dz
   out first and reflects through 2048 afterwards. Note that dz here is ALWAYS
   negative - the boils are the back wall - so the untested branch is the one
   that would run.

2. THE CUBES WERE DROPPED WHEN THEY CAME TOO CLOSE. af_cube() started as a copy
   of the additive-quad path, which rejects anything with otz below
   SCENE_OT_MIN because losing a band of a light is free. Losing a PUSS BALL is
   not: the one moment it has to be visible is the half second before it reaches
   the player, which is exactly when its otz is smallest. It clamps now.
   The same copy inherited the room mesh's +40 OT bias, which is there so an
   additive light lying on the floor does not fight the floor - and which would
   have sorted a landed boulder BEHIND the polygon it was standing on.

3. "NEVER HIGH ENOUGH TO GO OVER THE PLAYER'S HEAD" IS UNSATISFIABLE AS
   WRITTEN, and it is worth recording rather than quietly reinterpreting. The
   brief asks for a puss ball that arcs up and then down and never rises above
   the player's head. The boils are part of the back wall at y=-400 and y=-600,
   i.e. 400 and 600 above the floor; the player's eye is at y=-189. A ball
   leaving a boil is already well over their head, and the only arc obeying the
   letter of it is a pure descent with no rise - which the same sentence rules
   out. The rise is therefore RELATIVE to the launch point
   (AF_PUSS_RISE_V/AF_PUSS_GRAV), which keeps the flat readable arc the
   constraint was really about. Flagged to the user rather than hidden.

---- THE TWO ASSUMPTIONS, BOTH ONE CONSTANT TO REVERSE --------------------
a. THE VOMIT STOPS EXPOSING HIM 0.5 s BEFORE ITS CLIP ENDS. The brief gives that
   rule for the slam and says only "as soon as this attack starts Asag becomes
   exposed" for the vomit. AF_EXPOSE_LEAD.
b. (WAS: the slam's four boulder cells drawn from a block around the player.
   Superseded by PART 9A - there are two boulders now, at fixed cells, and the
   hit zone is Asag's third of the arena rather than the cubes themselves.)

---- WHAT TO CHECK IN THE EMULATOR ---------------------------------------
The runbook's STEP 12 list applies; these are the ones specific to this fight:
  [ ] A BOIL CAN BE SHOT AT ALL. If three rounds into a lit boil do nothing, it
      is ASAG_BOIL_CLEAR_BACKOFF and the z=2700 wall - see above.
  [ ] THE HEAD CANNOT BE HURT DURING THE LASER OR AN IDLE, and can during the
      slam, the vomit and the faint. The health bar turns GREEN while exposed,
      which is the read-out for exactly this.
  [ ] BURST BOTH BOILS DURING AN ATTACK. The attack must play out, THEN the
      faint, THEN that same attack again from the top.
  [ ] BURST BOTH DURING AN IDLE. The faint should come immediately, not after
      the idle's remaining four seconds.
  [ ] THE LASER LEAVES THE WHOLE LANDING THIRD BURNING, not a scribble under
      where the beam went, and it hurts for three seconds - once a second while
      stood in it, not once a frame.
  [ ] THE VOMIT'S LANE REACHES BOTH ENDS OF THE ROOM. Stand at the landing, in
      the middle of the arena, and right under the back wall, all on the centre
      line: all three must be hit. Then stand one column off it and be safe.
  [ ] THE TWO BOULDERS SMASH SEPARATELY. Two impacts, two bursts of chips. If
      you see one burst, the stagger is not working - every burst in
      src/particles.c overwrites the whole pool.
  [ ] THE WHOLE OF ASAG'S THIRD LIGHTS DURING THE SLAM, faintly, with the two
      boulder cells bright on top. Standing anywhere in that third when they
      land must hurt, not just under a cube.
  [ ] THE BODY FLASHES RED WHEN A SHOT LANDS and the bar is RED and only shows
      for two seconds after a hit. No green anywhere.
  [ ] KILL HIM FROM A CORNER. The death must put the player back in that corner,
      not at the landing. This is mistake 4 in the runbook and the reason
      begin_death() re-captures the camera rather than reusing the opening's.
  [ ] KILL HIM MID-SLAM, with his head on the floor 969 units out. He must
      return to Home before freezing - that is asag_ramp_home(), and without it
      the idle's "inherit the offset" rule freezes him mid-lunge.
  [ ] THE DEATH HAS SOUND. If it is silent, SFX_EXPLODE has lost its ASAG tag
      and nothing will say so - see PART 9A.
  [ ] HE GLOWS RED FOR THE WHOLE DEATH, unbroken from the killing blow through
      the settle, the freeze, the burn and the fade. A drop back to normal
      colour partway through means the flash is on hit_timer alone.
  [ ] OPEN THE INVENTORY MID-FIGHT AND LEAVE IT OPEN. He must keep attacking.
  [ ] FRAME RATE WITH A ZONE LIT. 75 large additive floor quads plus the
      particles is the heaviest thing in the fight; see the last section of
      PART 9A. tools/DIAGNOSING_FRAME_RATE.txt is the file, not guesswork -
      measure U/D/G first.



##########################################################################
 PART 9A - THE FIFTH PASS: HIT ZONES ARE THIRDS, NOT RADII
##########################################################################
The fourth pass built the fight with every attack measured as a RADIUS from
wherever the animator put Asag's head. It worked and it read badly, and the
reason is worth keeping because it is a general one.

>>> A BLAST RADIUS IS A NUMBER. A THIRD OF THE ROOM IS A PLACE. <<< A radius
has to be learned by being hit by it twice, it has no edge the player can see,
and because Asag cannot turn it was always centred on the same spot - so what
the player actually learned was "stand somewhere else", once, forever. Naming
the zones instead gives each attack a piece of the arena, lets the FLOOR say
which piece, and makes the three attacks compose into a loop.

---- THE GRID DIVIDES EXACTLY, WHICH IS WHY THIS WORKS AT ALL --------------
The arena floor is 15 columns x 15 rows (x -1500..1500 by 200, z 0..2800 by
186.67). Fifteen divides by three with nothing left over, so a third is five
rows or five columns and no rounding is involved anywhere:

    ROWS  0.. 4   z    0.. 933   THE LANDING END      the laser burns this
    ROWS  5.. 9   z  933..1867   the middle           only the vomit crosses it
    ROWS 10..14   z 1867..2800   ASAG'S END           the boulders fall here
    COLS  5.. 9   x -500.. 500   the centre lane      the vomit, end to end

>>> AND THE THREE COVER DIFFERENT GROUND, WHICH IS THE WHOLE LOOP. <<< The
laser drives the player FORWARD off the landing. The boulders drive them BACK
off Asag's end. The vomit splits the room LEFT and RIGHT. No spot in the arena
survives all three, so standing still is never the answer and the four-second
idles are when the player chooses where to be next. >>> CHANGE ONE ZONE AND
CHECK IT AGAINST THE OTHER TWO BEFORE BELIEVING IT. <<<

---- WHAT CHANGED, ATTACK BY ATTACK ---------------------------------------
THE LASER. Was an arc at a fixed range from his head, lighting the single cell
under its ground point. Now the ground point rakes STRAIGHT ACROSS in X at a
fixed z (AF_LAS_SWEEP_Z, the middle of the landing third), and as it crosses
each column it sets that column's whole five-row height alight. The sweep is a
WIPE: by the time it reaches the far wall the entire third is burning, and it
burns for three seconds.

  The separate beam-contact damage test is GONE, and that is a consequence
  rather than a simplification: the beam lights the player's whole column the
  instant it reaches them, so the trail test IS the contact test. Keeping both
  would have double-charged the one frame the beam arrives.

THE VOMIT. Was an 1100-unit circle under his mouth at z=1657 - which cannot
reach the front of the arena at all, so the whole attack was dodged by never
walking forward. It is the centre LANE now, wall to wall, and there is no
"far enough away" from it: the dodge is sideways. The particles were also
re-thrown - a large spread in Z and a small one in X, so the spray fans down
the lane it is about to poison instead of falling in a heap.

  The frozen-mouth machinery went with it. The circle used to be pinned at the
  first frame of the spray because the twitch moved his head 60 units eight
  times a second and a hit box that jitters is a hit box nobody can learn. The
  lane is fixed by the ROOM, so there is nothing to pin.

THE SLAM. Four random boulders became TWO fixed ones, at columns 4 and 10 (x
-600 and +600) in row 12. Symmetric about the room's centre line, which is also
symmetric about Asag - his head sits at x=-15 through the whole clip, so "one
to each side of him" and "one to each side of the room" are the same two cells
to within fifteen units.

  THEY SMASH. spawn_rock_burst() in src/particles.c is spawn_wood_burst with
  stone colours and square chunks instead of planks, which is what "similar to
  when we smash our Crate asset but coloured to suit the boulders" asks for. The
  shape is the only deviation and it is in the particle function rather than the
  caller, because the fracture belongs to the material.

  >>> AND THEY ARE STAGGERED BY NINE FRAMES, WHICH IS NOT DECORATION. <<<
  Every burst in src/particles.c fills the WHOLE pool and resets
  particle_count, so two smashes on the same frame show as ONE. The stagger is
  what buys two impacts and two bursts. It is implemented as a NEGATIVE start
  time so both boulders still fall for the same AF_T_BLD_FALL - shortening the
  second one's fall instead would have made it visibly faster than the first.

  THE DAMAGE IS THE THIRD, NOT THE CUBES. Two boulders 1200 apart cannot cover
  a 3000-wide room between them, and the design is that being caught in Asag's
  end when they land is what hurts. So the WHOLE THIRD lights faintly in the
  same light blue and the two cells a boulder is falling into light brightly on
  top of it - one effect at two intensities, saying "all of this is dangerous,
  and THOSE two are about to be hit by something". Lighting only the two cells
  would have been the brief read literally and would have been an ambush;
  lighting the third evenly would have thrown away the boulders' own telegraph.

---- THE FEEDBACK CHANGES ------------------------------------------------
THE HEALTH BAR IS THE RABISU'S AGAIN. The fourth pass raised it for the whole
of every exposure window and turned it GREEN, on the argument that "can I hurt
him now" is the question this fight asks every second. Dropped: a bar that comes
and goes on its own is a second UI element competing with the thing it hangs
over. Two seconds of a RED bar when something connects, and nothing otherwise.

THE BODY FLASHES RED INSTEAD, which is where that feedback belonged - it is on
the thing the player is aiming at rather than on a strip above it.
asag_set_hit_glow() is src/rabisu.c's exact modulation (R to full, G and B cut
to a quarter of whatever the fog left, applied AFTER the fog because it is light
coming out of him). src/asag_fight.c drives it every frame from
(hit_timer > 0 || dying), so it also runs UNBROKEN under the whole death rather
than expiring two seconds in.

THE DEATH GAINED ITS LIGHTS AND ITS SOUND.
  - FIVE glow points along the body, ramping on over the burn and riding the
    fade out. rbs_glow_point, the Rabisu's own primitive, because the two deaths
    are meant to read as one phenomenon.
    >>> WHERE THEY HANG IS THE ONE THING THAT COULD NOT BE COPIED. <<< The
    Rabisu hangs four on hand-measured mesh-local anchors pushed through its yaw
    and lean. Asag has no yaw, no lean and no head-and-wings - he is a
    1669-unit animal lying along the view axis. asag_span_point() slices the
    POSED body and returns a point in each slice, which needs no measurement and
    follows the pose, so the same call works whether he is stretched out at Home
    or flat on the floor.
  - The fade's colour is the RED he has been burning with, not a separate
    orange, so nothing switches colour halfway out.
  - SFX_EXPLODE, on the frame the lights start.

---- THE SOUND, AND THE ONE THING THAT WOULD HAVE BEEN SILENT -------------
>>> SFX_EXPLODE WAS BOSS-BANK ONLY, AND SND_BANK_ASAG IS THE ONLY BANK LOADED
IN THIS ARENA. <<< Played as it was, the entire death sequence would have run in
silence with no error anywhere - the exact failure mode at the head of
src/sound.c's bank table. It is tagged BOSS|ASAG now, one copy in each.

IT WAS FREE, and STEP 3 of tools/ADDING_A_SOUND.txt was re-run to prove it
rather than assumed:

                bank_base   region   house    boss   garden   intro    asag   spare
    before        0x46150   237232  162560  190336   177216   22080   54016   46896
    after         0x46150   237232  162560  190336   177216   22080   87872   46896

`spare` is the region less the LARGEST bank and BOSS still sets it, so filling
this bank stays free until it becomes the largest - about 100 KB away.

AND IT DOES NOT MOVE THE ARENA DOOR'S HEAP PEAK. sound_bank_select's read grows
from 55,296 bytes to 88,064, and it is FREED before main.c loads the boss model:
main.c puts the frees before the bank swap and the loads after it, deliberately
(PART 6). So the peak is still the 100,352-byte model read and not the sum.
>>> IF THOSE STEPS ARE EVER MERGED INTO ONE if/else IT BECOMES THE SUM. <<<

VOICE 21 is SFX_EXPLODE's own and is BOSS-bank, therefore guaranteed idle here,
so nothing in the burn can cut the clip short.

ABE_T_D_BURN + ABE_T_D_FADE = 232 + 90 = 322 frames = 5.37 s, WHICH IS THE
CLIP'S LENGTH. That was true by inheritance from the Rabisu before the clip was
played here and is true on purpose now. Retrim EXPLODE.VAG and both bosses'
pairs have to move; src/sound.h's entry for it says so.

---- WHAT IT COST --------------------------------------------------------
NOTHING ON THE DISC and nothing in VRAM, again: no new assets, and everything
the zones draw is untextured additive POLY_F4.

SPU: 33,856 bytes in the asag bank, free (above).

MAIN RAM: the fifth pass is roughly a wash - FREE AT REST went 176,216 ->
175,752, i.e. 464 bytes. The laser lost its arc maths and the slam lost its
random-cell search; the zone draws and spawn_rock_burst put it back.

---- THE ONE THING TO MEASURE RATHER THAN REASON ABOUT --------------------
>>> FILL RATE. <<< A lit zone is 75 additive floor quads (5 x 15 for the vomit's
lane, 5 x 15 for the slam's third, up to 75 for the laser's burn), and they are
large, overlapping and semi-transparent - which is the one cost profile this
game has been bitten by before. They cannot all be on at once (the laser's burn
has expired by the time the slam runs, and the attacks are four seconds apart),
so the worst case is one zone plus its particles.

Per-cell rather than per-row is deliberate: the same pixels either way, five
times the primitives, but rbs_glow_quad DROPS any quad with a projected vertex
past the GTE's +/-1023 clamp, so a row-wide quad would vanish entirely from a
camera standing in it where a cell-sized one loses only itself.

tools/DIAGNOSING_FRAME_RATE.txt is the file if it ever matters. Measure U/D/G
first; optimising this by reasoning made things 56% worse the last time it was
tried.

---- IT MATTERED, AND THE 75 ABOVE IS OUT OF DATE (Sep 2026) --------------
>>> THE SIXTH PASS GREW BOTH HEAVY ZONES TO 125 CELLS AND THIS SECTION WAS NOT
REVISITED. <<< Closing the arena's two safe squares turned the slam's third into
a C (75 + 50) and the vomit's lane into a plus (75 + 75 - 25). That is 67% more
of the one thing this section names as the fight's heaviest, and the fight was
then reported as lagging during certain attacks — which is exactly the pair of
attacks that grew.

WHAT THE ROOM ALREADY HAD. Checked against tools/DIAGNOSING_FRAME_RATE.txt's
three-item list before anything was written, because STEP 3D and STEP 3E both
found rooms missing all three. src/asag_arena.c has STEP 3C fix 1 (isin/icos and
the cull distance hoisted) and fix 2 (render_set_clear_colour, no second
full-screen tile). It deliberately has no cull-key table and says why: at 522
primitives inside a 5900-unit room nothing is ever rejected by distance, so the
table would cost BSS to answer a question that is always "yes". The room side of
that document is done; the FIGHT's own draws had none of it.

WHAT WENT IN: THE SIDE-PLANE CULL ON THE POOLS (src/asag_fight.c,
af_cell_on_screen). The room's own frustum test, run on a cell's four corners,
before rbs_glow_quad spends a gte_rtpt + gte_rtps + gte_avsz4 on a quad its
+/-1023 test is about to throw away. A cell is dropped only when ALL FOUR
corners are outside the SAME plane, so it covers no pixel and nothing that was
drawn stops being drawn. The trig is hoisted once per frame in asag_fight_draw
(STEP 3C fix 1 again, one level down). Level 5 turns it off for the A/B.

COUNTED OFFLINE over the walkable arena at 200-unit spacing and 16 headings, of
125 cells:

    slam    mean 27.8 visible, worst 107      78% culled on average
    vomit   mean 28.3 visible, worst 105      77% culled on average

>>> THE WORST POSE IS BARELY IMPROVED AND THAT IS THE HONEST HALF. <<< Standing
back and looking down a lit zone still queues 107 quads. This change is aimed at
CPU in the mean case; if the meter says the cost is FILL in the worst case, the
levers left are all look decisions — the zone's brightness, or how much of it is
lit at once, which is the Rabisu's 16-lights-to-12 all over again. Do not reach
for a row merge: the paragraph above is still right, and a row-wide quad
disappears entirely from a camera standing in it.

WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER FROM THE RABISU, so nobody writes it later. STEP 3C fix 3
is a BACKFACE CULL ON ADDITIVE GEOMETRY, and rbs_glow_quad_ex's own comment says
it is worth it for a CLOSED BOX of additive walls and nothing else — "the pool
quad and the glow-point rings are NOT closed boxes and must never take it; they
would simply disappear when viewed from the wrong side." Every additive shape in
this fight is a pool, a ring or a crossed beam pair. There is no shaft here and
PART 9A's opening explains why there must not be one.

>>> NOBODY HAS READ THE METER IN THIS ROOM YET. <<< Same honest note STEP 3D and
STEP 3E carry. The counting is what STEP 6 endorses and the change is
behaviour-neutral by construction, but U/D/G at levels 1, 5 and 8, standing in
the arena with a zone lit, is still owed.


##########################################################################
 PART 7A — COLLISION: THE ROOM IS A TABLE, THE BOSS IS NOT
##########################################################################
tools/gen_asag_arena_collision.py builds src/asag_arena_mesh_collision.c from
the room proxy alone: 12 walls and 3 floor planes against MAX_WALLS_PER_ROOM
128.

>>> THE BOSS WAS IN THAT TABLE FOR ONE PASS AND HAD TO COME OUT. THIS IS THE
MOST USEFUL THING IN THIS SECTION. <<< Its drawn geometry was converted to 26
static walls, on the reasoning that the mesh the player sees should be the mesh
the player cannot walk through. The walls were baked from frame 1 of
Head_Idle_Baked, where the body rests at y[-960,-528] — 528 above the floor and
342 above the player's eye — so collision.c's vertical gate rejected every one
of them and they never fired at all. They were, honestly, placeholders for the
day the art came down.

The old version of this file said so, and said what to do about it: "when the
encounter animates them, either accept the resting silhouette or replace those
walls with a runtime capsule the way rabisus_collide() does. Do NOT try to
re-bake walls per frame — CollisionRoom is a fixed table read by every movement
query in the frame."

THAT IS EXACTLY WHAT HAPPENED. The position track slides the body up to 969
units into the arena, and the slam and the faint bring it to floor level, so the
baked table went wrong in Z and in Y in the same pass. asag_collide() in
src/asag.c replaces it.

HOW THE RUNTIME COLLIDER WORKS, and the one idea in it worth keeping: it takes
an AABB over ONLY THE VERTICES INSIDE THE PLAYER'S VERTICAL BAND, from the same
posed vertices the draw uses, every frame. Selecting on Y FIRST is what makes a
single box enough. A box round the whole body would be 1669 long in Z and would
wall off a third of the arena whenever Asag leaned in, nearly all of it well
over the player's head. Selecting first means the box is EMPTY for most of the
fight — measured, the idle, the laser and the vomit never reach body height at
all — and small when it is not: the slam is solid for 3.9 of its 6.2 seconds and
the faint for 2.8 of its 5.0.

The band is widened by 60 units (ASAG_SOLID_Y_SLACK) because vertex spacing on
this model is 100-200 units against a 179-unit band, so a steeply descending
part could otherwise cross it with no vertex landing inside and let the player
walk through a leg. More slack than that starts fattening the box while the body
is merely passing overhead, which reads as an invisible barrier.

The rule in tools/ADDING_A_ROOM.txt applies in reverse throughout: read the
height off the mesh, and do not assume a boss occupies the ground just because
it is large. Asag spends most of the fight over the player's head.

>>> AND THE BLEND'S ACTION NAMES DO NOT ALL MATCH THE CLIP NAMES. <<< Four of
the six _Baked actions are named for their clip; two are not. The laser's is
Head_Laser_Baked and NOT Head_Attack_Laser_Baked, and the vomit's is
Head_Vomit_Baked. Both are unambiguous — one candidate each, frame ranges
matching their plain counterparts exactly — but the mapping is EXPLICIT in
export_asag.py's CLIPS table rather than derived, so a rename in the blend fails
with a KeyError instead of silently baking the rest pose.

The blend also stashes clips in NLA tracks. An unmuted stashed track OVERRIDES
the active action and the bake then produces the wrong clip with no error
anywhere; set_action() mutes them all. Blender 4.4+ actions are also SLOTTED,
and without a slot assignment the action is attached but evaluates to nothing —
which looks like a clip that exists and does not move.

WHO PLAYS WHAT NOW. The OPENING SCENE plays the faint (seeded two seconds in,
via asag_play_at) and then the idle, LOOPING — the one place in this boss a
`loop` argument of 1 is right, because the idle has to hold under the boils and
the whole of the speech, which is 14 seconds against the clip's 1.25. The
placeholder fight after the handover cycles the other four and then the idle
again, all NON-LOOPING.

NON-LOOPING IS WHAT MAKES A CHAIN WORK AT ALL: a looping clip never reports
asag_clip_done() and a cycle would stop on it forever. A non-looping play HOLDS
the last frame, which is what asag_clip_done() reports and what lets one clip
hand over to the next. The corollary is that the scene's own phases, which run
UNDER a looping idle, are all on frame counts instead.
