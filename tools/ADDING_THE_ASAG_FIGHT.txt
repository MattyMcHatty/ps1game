PS1 HORROR GAME — ASAG'S ARENA: WHAT IS RESERVED, AND WHAT IT COST
===================================================================
Asag is the game's SECOND boss.

textured mesh, its collision, seven textures, EIGHT drawable body parts and

##########################################################################
 PART 1 — WHAT THE ARENA IS, AND WHY ITS SHAPE IS LOAD-BEARING
##########################################################################
ONE WAY IN: the 1200-unit drop down the pit in The Hatch's yard, after both
keyholes are turned. That drop used to gate back into The Hatch itself as a
placeholder; it now lands in STATE_ASAG_ARENA.

ONE WAY OUT — still the rule, but WHERE it is has not been decided. The
placeholder arena had a door in the middle of the south wall; the real mesh has
no door anywhere (see THE REAL GEOMETRY below). No save point, and there will
not be one.

>>> EVERY BUDGET BELOW FOLLOWS FROM THAT AND FROM NOTHING ELSE. <<< Because
nothing else in the game is drawn, heard or updated while the player is down
there, and because the player cannot arrive from anywhere but the shaft:

  - almost all of VRAM may be overwritten, and nothing has to be put back;
  - the SPU bank can be empty of shared effects;
  - the encounter can seal the door for its whole length and know that winning
    and dying are the only two exits, so there is no re-entry case and no
    "replay or skip the reveal" question.

Change the room's shape — give it a second door, a save point, a way back up
the shaft — and every one of those goes away. That is the one decision in this
whole feature that is expensive to revisit.

THE REAL GEOMETRY, as built: x[-1500,1500] z[0,3698], floor y=0, perimeter
walls to y=-1000, with an ALCOVE cut into the back wall at x[-300,300]
z[2613,3248] which is where Asag's head sits.

>>> AND THE MESH HAS NO DOOR IN IT. <<< The alcove is the collision proxy's only
opening and it is the boss's throat. The exit prompt therefore sits at the SHAFT
MOUTH the player fell through (AA_EXIT_X/Z in src/asag_arena.c) and main.c's
destination for it is a placeholder too. That is the one thing about this room
that is still an open design question rather than an unbuilt one: ONE WAY OUT is
still the rule the budgets depend on, but where that way out IS has not been
decided or modelled.

 PART 2 — VRAM: 14 FULL 8bpp PAGES, PLUS THE 128-ROW SPRITE PAIR
##########################################################################
tools/VRAM_MAP_ASAG.txt is this room's map, generated by tools/vram_map_asag.py.
It is the third such map (after the whole-disc one and the garden-west bank's)
and by far the emptiest.

RESERVED — 21 textures, and they are exactly "what the player brought":
  the HUD panel, the bullet-impact sprite, entity drop shadows;
  the Crucifaxe, the Grave-olver, standard rounds, flame rounds, the

  every collectible menu icon (door key / copper pot, wax cube, the four key

  grdngtl + grdngtr — the GATE door panel, which BOTH the arrival and the exit

>>> THE DOOR PANEL IS THE SUBTLE ONE. <<< door_anim draws its panel after the

RECLAIMABLE — everything else, and unlike the garden-west bank most of it is
free rather than merely available: every mansion and garden room re-uploads its
own art on entry, and the arena is only ever left through a transition that runs
one of those uploaders. THE ARENA OWES NOBODY A RESTORE ON THE WAY OUT.

WHAT THAT LEAVES:
  14 full 8bpp 128x128 pages at Voff 0. Six of them cost nothing at all; the
  other eight hold a startup-resident texture and taking one obliges you to
  restore it (the map names which, per page).
  5 more pages are 4bpp-only (their right half holds a reserved menu icon).
  AND the whole Voff-128 band, including x320/x384 y128 — the shared
  spider/rafflesia sprite pair, 128 rows by 128 columns, the largest contiguous
  hole in the bank. main.c's STATE_LOADING branch SKIPS that upload for this

  It is the right shape for a boss sprite sheet.

For comparison: the Greenhouse, the most texture-hungry room in the game, owns
six. There is room here for roughly twice that.

##########################################################################
 PART 3 — SPU: THE WHOLE 232 KB REGION, AND HOW IT WAS PAID FOR
##########################################################################
SND_BANK_ASAG (bit 16, src/sound.h) exists and is EMPTY. main.c's STATE_LOADING
selects it for STATE_ASAG_ARENA and for nothing else.

An empty bank is the point. The arena can hold no monster, so not one shared
effect has to be carried, and everything the PLAYER makes a noise with is
RESIDENT and unaffected by which bank is in:

    SWING  AXEHIT  GR_SHOT  GR_RELOAD  HURT  DIE  STEP1  STEP2
    PICKUP  SMASH  SLAM  UNLOCK  DOOR  RBS_SWING  CURSOR  SELECT  BACK

That list is already complete. Nothing needs adding to the bank to make the
player work; every byte of it is Asag's.

>>> THE REGION WAS GROWN BY 47 KB TO GET THERE, AND IT IS WORTH KNOWING HOW. <<<
The Rabisu's FIREBALL, BOOM and EXPLODE were SND_RESIDENT — 47,808 bytes — on
the reasoning that they fire mid-fight and a bank swap is a CD read. That
reasoning was sound and the conclusion was wrong: all three are played by
src/rabisu.c and src/rabisu_boss.c alone, world.c places a Rabisu in the Garden
Courtyard alone, and SND_BANK_BOSS is loaded for the whole of that room. The
bank was already making the guarantee the residency was paying for. And a
resident clip is charged TWICE — once against the permanent block, and again
against the region, which is whatever is left after it.

They are SND_BANK_BOSS now. Before and after, from STEP 3 of
tools/ADDING_A_SOUND.txt:

                    bank_base   region    house    boss   garden   intro   asag   spare
    before          0x51C10     189424   162560  142528  177216   22080     -    12208
    after           0x46150     237232   162560  190336  177216   22080     0    46896

So every bank in the game gained 47 KB of ceiling and `spare` nearly quadrupled.
GARDEN is still the largest bank and still the one that sets `spare`.

>>> DO NOT REPEAT THE MISTAKE WITH ASAG. <<< Its arena is a one-way pocket whose
bank is loaded for the entire room, cutscene and fight alike, so an effect that
plays mid-fight belongs in SND_BANK_ASAG and NOT in the residents. A resident
clip would cost the whole game twice over for a room it can only ever be heard
in. Re-run the STEP 3 arithmetic with an `asag` list before writing any code —
an overflowing bank is SILENT, not broken.

Voices: 0..23, and Asag inherits a crowded map (sfx_channel in src/sound.c).
Voices 19, 20, 21 and 22 are EMERGE, DMNSPEAK, EXPLODE and BOOM — all BOSS-bank
now, so all four are guaranteed idle in the arena and are the natural borrowings
for Asag's long clips, exactly as SFX_HAD_DIE and SFX_WATER borrow them today.
13, 14 and 15 (GAS, PULL, HISS) are GARDEN-bank and likewise idle here.


##########################################################################
 PART 4 — MUSIC
##########################################################################
CDAUDIO_ASAG_TRACK = 9 is reserved in src/cdaudio.h. The audio is NOT on the
disc: adding it is one <track type="audio"> line APPENDED at the foot of
disc.xml, 44100 Hz 16-bit stereo. A track inserted rather than appended
renumbers every track after it and every room plays the wrong music.

>>> IT IS THE BOSS'S TRACK, NOT THE ROOM'S. <<< main.c's STATE_LOADING branch
calls cdaudio_stop() for this room, so the arena is silent before the encounter
and after it, and the phase machine starts the track at the beat the brief
names. Do not replace that stop with nothing: a title-screen load or a debug
jump into the arena does not pass through the drop's own stop.


##########################################################################
 PART 4A — THE DISC DIRECTORY, WHICH IS NOT WHERE YOU EXPECT
##########################################################################
ASAG'S THIRTY FILES ARE IN \TEXASAG\, NOT \TEX\, and both halves of that name
are load-bearing.

WHY NOT \TEX\: its ISO9660 record already spans FIVE 2048-byte sectors, and the
note at the head of src/chain_room.c records a boot crash caused by growing it by
TWO files - the game died inside sound_init, in the first CdSearchFile that
switched from \TEX\ to \SND\, with 200 KB of heap spare and a clean VRAM map.
Putting Asag's files there took it to six sectors. A directory of its own costs
ONE record in the root, starts again at sector 1, and leaves room for the
fight's sounds and clips.

WHY "TEXASAG" AND NOT "ASAG": >>> mkpsxiso SORTS THE ROOT. <<< It writes root
records in identifier order, not in disc.xml order, and SYSTEM.CNF is the LAST
record in the root's first sector with 36 bytes to spare. A directory called
ASAG sorts BEFORE it and pushes it past byte 2048, at which point the boot ROM
cannot find it. tools/check_disc_root.py said so immediately:

    SYSTEM.CNF;1   record [2048,2108)  *** PAST 2048: WILL NOT BOOT ***

TEXASAG sorts after SYSTEM.CNF (and right after TEX), so its record lands in the
root's second sector where 1.5 KB is free. RUN check_disc_root.py AFTER ANY
CHANGE to disc.xml.

The same pressure is why the six boils ship as ONE joined mesh
(tools/export_asag.py): they never animate and are always drawn together, so six
records bought nothing.

##########################################################################
 PART 6 — MAIN RAM: THE CONSTRAINT THAT DECIDES EVERYTHING
##########################################################################
IT IS NOT VRAM AND IT IS NOT THE SPU. Read tools/HEAP_BUDGET.txt before doing
anything that allocates.

WHAT THE ARENA COST. 12,316 bytes:
    ~9,700 bytes   one more RoomState in world.c's BSS. Every room in the game
                   pays this; it is the fixed-capacity entity arrays, and the
                   arena's copy will hold nothing but zeroes forever.
     2,649 bytes   the two new modules' code and BSS.
The room's own textures cost NOTHING — they stream on entry into a scratch that
is freed — and it registers nothing with texmgr. That was deliberate: six
registrations would have been ~110 KB and would not have booted.

>>> AND THEN 104,448 BYTES WERE RECLAIMED, BECAUSE THE BOSS MODEL WOULD NOT
    OTHERWISE HAVE FITTED. <<<

The Rabisu held its model and its one clip from startup and never released them:

    RBSIDLE.PVA    75,776 bytes    ONE idle clip, 19 frames x 496 verts
    RABISU.SMD     28,672 bytes    the model
                  ---------
                  104,448 bytes    for a boss that exists in exactly one room

Asag is specified with animationS, plural, and tools/ANIMATING_A_3D_MODEL.txt
says the .pva cost scales linearly per clip. A model plus three or four clips is
150–250 KB. Against the 245 KB the tree had at that point — with a MEASURED boot
cliff at ~234 KB, and a failure mode that is not a tidy malloc error but the
stack being handed out — that was simply not going to boot.

So the Rabisu's two buffers are now ROOM-SCOPED, which is the move
src/room_arena.c already made for room meshes: exactly one boss is ever live,
which one is known at the transition, and the transition already makes several
bracketed CD reads behind a black screen. main.c's STATE_LOADING calls
rabisus_load_model() when the destination is the Garden Courtyard and
rabisus_free_model() on every other transition, unconditionally and keyed on
pending_area — the same contract sound_bank_select() has.

THE HEAP, not BSS, and that is the whole point: unlike a room, NO boss is loaded
almost all of the time, so a BSS block would cost its full size in every room in
the game. Freeing is what turns 104 KB spent into 104 KB free.

WHERE IT LEAVES THINGS:

    FREE AT REST    371,932 bytes (363 KB), 31.4% of the heap
    ROOM-SCOPED     165,888 bytes total, largest single item 75,776

That is the most headroom this heap has had since before the Greenhouse, and it
is 137 KB clear of the measured cliff. (16 KB of the improvement is a REPORTING
correction, not a reclaim: heap_budget.py used to count chainlink_door.c's and
grinder.c's local TIM scratch as permanent. See the note in that script.)

WHAT MADE THE RABISU CHANGE SAFE — repeat this audit before doing the same to
any other entity:
  - rabisu_smd and rabisu_anim_frames are read by ONE thing, the draw, and every
    path to them is behind draw_rabisus()'s existing
    `if (!rabisu_smd || rabisu_count == 0) return;`
  - update_rabisus(), rabisus_collide() and rabisu_anchor_world() touch no model
    data; the first two skip anything whose r->area is not current_area
  - rabisu_add() — which world_seed_room() runs for rooms that are NOT loaded —
    reads nothing from the mesh either
With the model unloaded the boss is simply not drawn, which is the state every
room but the courtyard was already in.

>>> AND THE ORDER OF THOSE CALLS IS LOAD-BEARING. IT COST A CRASH TO FIND. <<<
Wiring the model up naively — one if/else, before the sound bank swap — made the
Garden Courtyard die on entry with the signature in
tools/DIAGNOSING_A_BOOT_CRASH.txt section 2. That door is the TIGHTEST MOMENT IN
THE GAME: it is the only transition that loads SND_BANK_BOSS, whose EMERGE clip
is a 71,680-byte malloc (the largest single transient in the program), and the
104,448-byte model was being loaded alongside it. 176,128 bytes live at one
instant, which is also what the old startup-resident code cost there — and the
arena's 12,316 permanent bytes were enough to tip it over.

So main.c SEQUENCES them, and the free and the load are deliberately NOT one
if/else:

    /* before the bank swap */
    if (pending_area != STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_free_model();
    ...
    sound_bank_select(...);
    /* after it */
    if (pending_area == STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_load_model();

Free early so the outgoing model is gone before the incoming bank is read; load
late so the bank's buffer is already freed. Peak at that door: 104,448 instead
of 176,128 — seventy kilobytes below what the shipped code always reached there.

>>> WHAT THIS MEANS FOR ASAG. <<< Do NOT give it a startup load, and put its
calls in the SAME TWO PLACES, not in one:

    /* before the bank swap */
    if (pending_area != STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_free_model();
    if (pending_area != STATE_ASAG_ARENA)       asags_free_model();
    ...
    sound_bank_select(...);
    /* after it */
    if (pending_area == STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_load_model();
    if (pending_area == STATE_ASAG_ARENA)       asags_load_model();

Asag's bank is EMPTY today, so its transition peak is its model alone — but that
is a property of the bank being empty and it stops being true the moment the
fight has clips. Work the peak out by hand: permanent + the largest malloc live
at that instant. FREE AT REST does not model it.

Because the two bosses can never be co-resident, Asag's budget is the whole
363 KB minus its own transient peak — comfortably enough for a model and several
clips. Keep an eye on tools/ANIMATING_A_3D_MODEL.txt all the same: 75 KB for one
19-frame clip is what the baked-vertex approach costs, and four of those is
300 KB.

STILL OUTSTANDING, and each is small:
  - the Rabisu's SKIN (RABISU.TIM, 18 KB) is still a startup texmgr
    registration. It could be streamed like the arena's textures, but its entry
    hook (garden_courtyard_upload_textures) is also reached from the Stables and
    the Greenhouse, so a naive change puts a CD read on two transitions that do
    not need one. Solve that first if the 18 KB is wanted.
  - the ~9,700-byte RoomState per room is paid by all 27 rooms whether or not
    they hold anything. That is a world.c refactor, not a boss one.

##########################################################################
 PART 6A — >>> heap_budget.py WAS WRONG BY 145 KB, AND IT COST THIS BOSS <<<
##########################################################################
READ THIS BEFORE BELIEVING ANY EARLIER FIGURE IN THIS FILE.

Every "371 KB free" and "363 KB free" above was produced by a script that
computed the heap as everything from _end to 0x801FFFF8 and subtracted only
texmgr and the kept buffers. THE TOP 145 KB OF THAT SPAN IS NOT HEAP. src/main.c
declares

    RenderContext ctx;

as a LOCAL in main(), and a RenderContext is two RenderBuffers - each an
OT_LENGTH(2048)-entry uint32 table plus a BUFFER_LENGTH(65536) packet buffer -
so main()'s stack frame is ~147,600 bytes and it sits at the top of RAM for the
entire run. The heap can only ever grow to just underneath it.

    reported FREE AT REST   361,212
    stack reserve          -148,152
    ---------------------------------
    real FREE AT REST       213,060      (208 KB)
    measured free at the arena door      ~171,368

WHAT IT COST. Asag's first build was sized against the phantom figure: 229 KB of
meshes and clips at half rate, which "fit" 361 KB with 110 KB to spare. The
console crashed on EVERY entry to the arena, by the drop and by the debug menu
alike. Headless, with the model reads instrumented:

    ASGHLAS.PVA -> buffer 0x801D46D0 .. 0x801DE6D0
    sp = 0x801DBD40                      <- the stack pointer is INSIDE it
    Attempted unaligned JR to 0xfe51ffbd from 0x8008D9A0
    -> CdReadSync, cdread.c:160

That is precisely the mechanism tools/HEAP_BUDGET.txt and section 2 of
tools/DIAGNOSING_A_BOOT_CRASH.txt describe: malloc does not fail when the heap
fills, it hands back memory the stack is using and the next CdRead DMAs through
a return address.

>>> AND IT EXPLAINS THE "~234 KB CLIFF" THAT NEVER MADE SENSE. <<< That figure
was measured in the old units. 234 KB reported was about 86 KB real, which is
why a heap with "226 KB free" would not boot. The cliff was never mysterious;
the ruler was wrong.

WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT:
  - tools/heap_budget.py now subtracts a MEASURED stack floor (STACK_FLOOR, read
    as $sp inside read_file on this very transition) and prints the reserve as
    its own line. Re-measure it the same way if main()'s frame ever changes.
  - src/asag.c's read_file() reads $sp and REFUSES any allocation that would
    reach within 8 KB of it. A refused read leaves a part or a clip absent - a
    bind pose, or nothing drawn - which is visible and harmless. It can no
    longer corrupt the stack.
  - the clips were re-baked at STEP 4.
  - the model load moved after the texture stream, so their peaks do not add.

>>> THE STACK RESERVE IS NOT RECOVERABLE BY MOVING ctx TO BSS. <<< It would move
the same 145 KB from the top of RAM to the bottom and shrink the heap by exactly
as much. What it WOULD do is make the accounting self-evident instead of
depending on a measured constant. Worth doing; not a win.


##########################################################################
 PART 7A — COLLISION, WHICH IS THREE MESHES AND NOT ONE
##########################################################################
tools/gen_asag_arena_collision.py merges smx_to_collision.py's output for THREE
meshes into src/asag_arena_mesh_collision.c:

    Asag_Arena mesh.smx       walls  0..7    the room proxy + the head alcove
    Asag_Left_Tentacle.smx    walls  8..27   the left arm's own geometry
    Asag_Right_Tentacle.smx   walls 28..47   the right arm's

48 walls against MAX_WALLS_PER_ROOM 128. The two arms are there because they lie
along the side walls and the player must not walk through them.

>>> THE TENTACLE WALLS ARE STATIC AND THE TENTACLES ARE NOT. <<< They are baked
from frame 1 of Tentacle_Idle, where each arm lies low (y span [-204,-4]). The
attack clip rears them through 1464 units of travel and none of that moves these
walls. That is the right trade while the fight does not exist; when it does,
either accept the resting silhouette or replace those walls with a runtime
capsule the way rabisus_collide() does. Do NOT try to re-bake walls per frame -
CollisionRoom is a fixed table read by every movement query in the frame.


##########################################################################
 PART 7B — THE ART PIPELINE, AND THE TWO BUGS IT CAUGHT
##########################################################################
ONE COMMAND RE-EXPORTS EVERYTHING:

    blender -b "blender models/Garden/Asag.blend" -P tools/export_asag.py
    smxlink -tp textures -o assets/bosses/Asag/<X>.smd assets/garden/asag/<X>.smx
    py tools/gen_asag_arena_tex_map.py
    py assets/smx_to_collision.py ... && py tools/gen_asag_arena_collision.py

export_asag.py exists because thirteen meshes and fourteen clips have to agree
with each other, and two of the agreements are easy to break by hand. It sets
each part's IDLE action and frame 1 before exporting its mesh, so the .smd's bind
pose IS frame 1 of the clip that part rests on (mistake 2 in
tools/ANIMATING_A_3D_MODEL.txt: the Rabisu's shipped mesh sat 216 units off its
clip's first frame and playback snapped). Verified: every clip's frame 1 matches
its .smd exactly, except head_emerge, which starts buried and is meant to.

TWO REAL BUGS IN THE ART, both found by tools/check_model_winding.py:

  1. ALL SIX BOILS AND THE RIGHT TENTACLE WERE WOUND BACKWARDS - the mirror
     signature from ANIMATING_A_3D_MODEL.txt mistake 1, so the draw's backface
     cull would have shown their insides.
     >>> AND "Recalculate Outside" DID NOT FIX THEM. <<< Every one is an OPEN
     mesh (the boils are 5-quad boxes with the wall-facing side deleted; the
     tentacles are capped tubes with 4 open edges), and for an open mesh
     "outside" is ambiguous - bmesh picks an orientation and both are
     defensible. The right tentacle had a second cause on top: a -1 determinant
     world matrix, being the mirror of the left. FLIPPING THE FACES fixes both,
     and it was done at source in the .blend.

  2. The blend carries DUPLICATE ACTIONS, plain and "_Baked". export_asag.py
     bakes both and diffs them rather than assuming. Head pairs are identical in
     motion (sub-unit rounding apart). Teneacle_Left_Baked and
     Tentacle_Right_Baked are NOT the attack clips - they travel 89 units where
     the real ones travel ~1470 - so the plain *_Attack actions are the ones
     that ship.

>>> AND THE ARENA MESH READS "INSIDE-OUT" TO check_model_winding.py, WHICH IS
CORRECT. <<< So do chain_room.smd and every other room: a room is viewed from
inside. That tool's OK/inverted verdict applies to PROPS AND MODELS. Compare a
new room mesh against an existing one before believing it.
