##########################################################################
 PART 1 — WHAT THE ARENA IS, AND WHY ITS SHAPE IS LOAD-BEARING
##########################################################################
ONE WAY IN: the 1200-unit drop down the pit in The Hatch's yard, after both
keyholes are turned. main.c takes that transition at hatch_puzzle_drop_done().

>>> AND, AS OF THIS PASS, NO WAY OUT. <<< The first arena had a placeholder exit
standing at the shaft mouth, offering to climb a shaft the fiction says cannot
be climbed, with main.c's destination for it gating back into The Hatch and
flagged as a placeholder in as many words. The new mesh has no door in it
either, and where the room actually leads has still not been decided — so rather
than ship the lie a second time, the exit is GONE: no prompt, no trigger, no
destination, and no asag_arena_exit_* API at all.

The only ways out of the room today are the DEBUG LEVEL SELECT (the "ASAG ARENA"
row, which is back in title.c for exactly this reason) and dying. A save made
BEFORE the drop is the only route back into the rest of the game. That is a
deliberate hole, it is written down in src/asag_arena.h, and it is the first
thing to fix when the fight is real.

>>> EVERY BUDGET BELOW FOLLOWS FROM THAT ONE-WAY SHAPE AND FROM NOTHING ELSE.
<<< Because nothing else in the game is drawn, heard or updated while the player
is down there, and because the player cannot arrive from anywhere but the shaft:

  - almost all of VRAM may be overwritten, and nothing has to be put back;
  - the SPU bank can be empty of shared effects;
  - the encounter can seal the door for its whole length and know that winning
    and dying are the only two exits, so there is no re-entry case and no
    "replay or skip the reveal" question.

Give the room a second door, a save point, or a way back up the shaft and every
one of those goes away. That is the one decision in this whole feature that is
expensive to revisit.

THE REAL GEOMETRY, as built: x[-1500,1500] z[0,2900], floor y=0, perimeter walls
to y=-1000, mesh detail up to y=-1534. The collision proxy is a perimeter with a
RECESS AT EACH END, both floored at y=0 and both narrower than the middle
(x[-1300,1300] against x[-1500,1500]):

    FLOOR 0   y=0   x[-1300,1300]  z[2613,2700]   the SOUTH recess, under Asag
    FLOOR 1   y=0   x[-1300,1300]  z[   0, 186]   the NORTH recess, the landing
    FLOOR 2   y=0   x[-1500,1500]  z[ 186,2613]   the arena floor itself

The player lands at x=0 z=300 facing +Z, straight down the arena at Asag.

>>> AND THE BOSS IS NOT IN THE ROOM. <<< Its bind pose spans z[2262,3931] —
a thousand units BEHIND the arena's own back wall — and y[-960,-528], so its
lowest point is 528 above the floor. Asag hangs over the arena out of the rock
behind it. Two consequences worth knowing before touching anything:

  - the cull distance is 7000 and NOT the room's own 5900 Manhattan diagonal,
    because a cull tight to the room would clip the far end of the boss off;
  - none of the boss's own collision walls can ever fire at rest (PART 7A).


##########################################################################
 PART 2 — VRAM: THREE TEXTURES, AND TWELVE FULL PAGES STILL FREE
##########################################################################
tools/VRAM_MAP_ASAG.txt is this room's map, generated by tools/vram_map_asag.py.
It is the third such map (after the whole-disc one and the garden-west bank's)
and by far the emptiest.

RESERVED — and it is exactly "what the player brought": the HUD panel, the
bullet-impact sprite, entity drop shadows; the Crucifaxe, the Grave-olver,
standard rounds, flame rounds, the Helluminator; the small medkit; every
collectible menu icon; and grdngtl + grdngtr, the GATE door panel.

>>> THE DOOR PANEL WAS THE SUBTLE ONE, AND IT IS NOW RESERVED AT ALL. <<<
door_anim draws its panel after the OUTGOING room's update and BEFORE
STATE_LOADING, so whichever panel a transition uses has to be in VRAM across it.
The arrival used to borrow DOOR_PANEL_GATE, which reserved grdngtl and grdngtr
in this bank — and meant a 1200-unit fall down a shaft was announced by a garden
gate creaking open.

The arrival is DOOR_PANEL_FALL now: four mud quads, flat in a 2x2 square, a long
way down the view axis, with the camera accelerating into them on t^2 and fading
to black before it arrives. Its texture is THIS ROOM'S OWN MUD, already owned at
x384 y0, so the bank reserves no panel any more.

>>> AND IT INVERTS THE USUAL RULE, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KNOWING. <<< Every
other panel in the game is in VRAM before its transition starts — put there once
at startup, or (the greenhouse door) guaranteed because both ends of the
transition upload it. Neither is true here: at the moment the fall begins, the
arena's uploader has not run and The Hatch's art is still at x384 y0. So
door_anim_start() streams the one TIM itself, through
asag_arena_upload_mud() — which owns the DrawSync(0) and the cdaudio bracket
that make a mid-game LoadImage legal (PROBLEM A in tools/TEXTURING_NOTES.txt).
It goes into the same rect and the same recorded tpage/clut the room will use,
which is why nothing new is reserved, and main.c calls cdaudio_stop() BEFORE
door_anim_start() rather than after so the bracket's resume cannot restart The
Hatch's music under the fall.

WHEN THE ROOM GROWS AN EXIT, whatever panel that uses comes back onto
RESERVED in tools/vram_map_asag.py.

RECLAIMABLE — everything else, and most of it is free rather than merely
available: every mansion and garden room re-uploads its own art on entry. THE
ARENA OWES NOBODY A RESTORE ON THE WAY OUT — trivially so today, since it has no
way out.

WHAT THIS BANK TAKES. THREE pages, all reclaimable, none owing a restore:

    mud        x384 y0    8bpp   the arena floor and its mud banks
    Boss Wall  x512 y0    8bpp   the perimeter
    asag       x640 y0    8bpp   THE BOSS'S SKIN — streamed by the ROOM

That leaves TWELVE full 8bpp pages plus the whole Voff-128 band, including
x320/x384 y128 — the shared spider/rafflesia sprite pair, 128 rows by 128
columns, the largest contiguous hole in the bank. main.c's STATE_LOADING branch
SKIPS that upload for this room specifically, so it is honestly free rather than
merely intended to be. It is the right shape for a boss sprite sheet.

For comparison: the Greenhouse, the most texture-hungry room in the game, owns
six pages. There is room here for twice that.

>>> THE BOSS'S SKIN IS IN THE ROOM'S TABLE, NOT REGISTERED BY THE BOSS. <<<
tools/ADDING_A_3D_ENEMY.txt STEP 3 says a textured enemy registers through
texmgr, which is right for the Rabisu and wrong here: that is ~17 KB of
PERMANENT main RAM for art that can only be seen behind a one-way drop, and this
room already makes a bracketed CD read on the only transition that reaches it.
Zero permanent bytes instead. src/asag.c reads the tpage/clut back through
asag_arena_tex_page()/asag_arena_tex_clut().

>>> ALL THREE TEXTURES ARE 128x128, AND THAT IS THE SMX'S DECISION, NOT THE
ENGINE'S. <<< The source art in textures/asag/ is three different sizes (asag
128, Boss wall 256, mud 64). io_export_smx_v3.py normalises every UV in an
export to exp_tex_size=128 regardless of the image it came from, and the arena's
own UVs prove it — mud runs u[10..147] and Boss Wall u[7..117], both addressed
over a 128-texel tile. Ship mud at its native 64 and UV 127 reads off its right
edge into whatever sits next in VRAM; ship Boss Wall at 256 and only its
top-left quarter is ever sampled. So the three are RESAMPLED to 128x128 on the
way from textures/asag/*.png to textures/*.png, and png_to_tim.py runs on those.

  Going genuinely bigger is possible and is not worth it: 256 forces 4bpp (16
  colours), a whole VRAM tpage across BOTH Voff halves, and a per-poly DR_TWIN
  bracket. tools/TEXTURING_NOTES.txt PART 5 has the full cost, and records that
  the one 256x256 attempt in this game — the Greenhouse's cuniform pipes — was
  reverted after 16 of its 40 faces straddled a tile edge and lost their art.

>>> AND MIND THE CLUT BAND. <<< The first pass put three CLUTs at x512 in the
y>=480 band, where they landed inside ddog_alert.tim's PIXELS — the exact trap
ADDING_A_3D_ENEMY.txt STEP 3 warns about. Do not reason about that band by hand:
place the TIM, regenerate tools/VRAM_MAP.txt and read its CLUT OVERLAP CHECK.
The three live at 672,499 / 672,500 / 672,501.

ADDING ONE: put the .tim in textures/, add a <file> to disc.xml's TEXASAG dir
(NOT the TEX dir — see PART 4A), add a row to stream_tex_file[] in
src/asag_arena.c, a row to ASAG_TEX_* in src/asag_arena.h, a row to BANK in
tools/vram_map_asag.py and, if an arena polygon uses it, a row to NAME_TO_SLOT
in gen_asag_arena_tex_map.py. Then regenerate BOTH maps. No generator run for
tim_slots.h — this room captures tpage/clut from the TIM at read time precisely
so a texture can be added without one.


##########################################################################
 PART 3 — SPU: THE WHOLE 232 KB REGION, AND HOW IT WAS PAID FOR
##########################################################################
SND_BANK_ASAG (bit 16, src/sound.h) exists and holds ONE CLIP. main.c's
STATE_LOADING selects it for STATE_ASAG_ARENA and for nothing else.

>>> THE ONE CLIP IS SFX_DMNSPEAK, AND IT IS TAGGED BOSS|ASAG. <<< The opening
scene puts one line of the demon speech over each of its two subtitles, exactly
as the Rabisu's reveal does, and the two bosses sit in different banks — so the
clip carries a copy in each, the same two-copies answer SFX_GATE and SFX_RUMBLE
already take. It cost nothing anybody will notice:

                    bank_base   region    house    boss   garden   intro   asag   spare
    before          0x46150     237232   162560  190336  177216   22080      0    46896
    after           0x46150     237232   162560  190336  177216   22080  54016    46896

`spare` is the region less the LARGEST bank, and BOSS still sets it. So filling
this bank stays free until it becomes the largest, which for Asag means another
136 KB of clips.

AND IT DID NOT MOVE THE ARENA DOOR'S HEAP PEAK EITHER, which is the constraint
that actually binds down there (PART 6). sound_bank_select's read is a
55,296-byte malloc that is FREED before main.c loads the boss model, and main.c
sequences the two apart on purpose — so the peak is still the 108,544-byte model
read rather than the sum. Re-do that arithmetic when the fight's own clips land;
PART 6's warning below was written for exactly this and it has now been paid
once without cost, which is not a guarantee about the next one.

An almost-empty bank is still the point. The arena can hold no monster, so not
one shared effect has to be carried, and everything the PLAYER makes a noise
with is RESIDENT and unaffected by which bank is in:

    SWING  AXEHIT  GR_SHOT  GR_RELOAD  HURT  DIE  STEP1  STEP2
    PICKUP  SMASH  SLAM  UNLOCK  DOOR  RBS_SWING  CURSOR  SELECT  BACK

That list is already complete. Nothing needs adding to the bank to make the
player work; every byte of it is Asag's.

>>> THE REGION WAS GROWN BY 47 KB TO GET THERE, AND IT IS WORTH KNOWING HOW. <<<
The Rabisu's FIREBALL, BOOM and EXPLODE were SND_RESIDENT — 47,808 bytes — on
the reasoning that they fire mid-fight and a bank swap is a CD read. That
reasoning was sound and the conclusion was wrong: all three are played by
src/rabisu.c and src/rabisu_boss.c alone, world.c places a Rabisu in the Garden
Courtyard alone, and SND_BANK_BOSS is loaded for the whole of that room. The
bank was already making the guarantee the residency was paying for. And a
resident clip is charged TWICE — once against the permanent block, and again
against the region, which is whatever is left after it.

They are SND_BANK_BOSS now. Before and after, from STEP 3 of
tools/ADDING_A_SOUND.txt:

                    bank_base   region    house    boss   garden   intro   asag   spare
    before          0x51C10     189424   162560  142528  177216   22080     -    12208
    after           0x46150     237232   162560  190336  177216   22080     0    46896

So every bank in the game gained 47 KB of ceiling and `spare` nearly quadrupled.
GARDEN is still the largest bank and still the one that sets `spare`.

>>> DO NOT REPEAT THE MISTAKE WITH ASAG. <<< Its arena is a one-way pocket whose
bank is loaded for the entire room, cutscene and fight alike, so an effect that
plays mid-fight belongs in SND_BANK_ASAG and NOT in the residents. A resident
clip would cost the whole game twice over for a room it can only ever be heard
in. Re-run the STEP 3 arithmetic with an `asag` list before writing any code —
an overflowing bank is SILENT, not broken.

Voices: 0..23, and Asag inherits a crowded map (sfx_channel in src/sound.c).
Voices 19, 20, 21 and 22 are EMERGE, DMNSPEAK, EXPLODE and BOOM — all BOSS-bank
now, so all four are guaranteed idle in the arena and are the natural borrowings
for Asag's long clips. 13, 14 and 15 (GAS, PULL, HISS) are GARDEN-bank and
likewise idle here.

##########################################################################
 PART 4A — THE DISC DIRECTORY, WHICH IS NOT WHERE YOU EXPECT
##########################################################################
ASAG'S TEN FILES ARE IN \TEXASAG\, NOT \TEX\, and both halves of that name are
load-bearing.

WHY NOT \TEX\: its ISO9660 record already spans FIVE 2048-byte sectors, and the
note at the head of src/chain_room.c records a boot crash caused by growing it by
TWO files — the game died inside sound_init, in the first CdSearchFile that
switched from \TEX\ to \SND\, with 200 KB of heap spare and a clean VRAM map. A
directory of its own costs ONE record in the root, starts again at sector 1, and
leaves room for the fight's sounds and clips. The second pass took it from 32
records to 13, i.e. 764 of 2048 bytes used — there is room for the fight.

WHY "TEXASAG" AND NOT "ASAG": >>> mkpsxiso SORTS THE ROOT. <<< It writes root
records in identifier order, not in disc.xml order, and SYSTEM.CNF is the LAST
record in the root's first sector with 36 bytes to spare. A directory called
ASAG sorts BEFORE it and pushes it past byte 2048, at which point the boot ROM
cannot find it. tools/check_disc_root.py said so immediately:

    SYSTEM.CNF;1   record [2048,2108)  *** PAST 2048: WILL NOT BOOT ***

TEXASAG sorts after SYSTEM.CNF (and right after TEX), so its record lands in the
root's second sector where 1.5 KB is free. RUN check_disc_root.py AFTER ANY
CHANGE to disc.xml.

##########################################################################
 PART 6 — MAIN RAM: THE CONSTRAINT THAT DECIDES EVERYTHING
##########################################################################
IT IS NOT VRAM AND IT IS NOT THE SPU. Read tools/HEAP_BUDGET.txt before doing
anything that allocates.

    FREE AT REST    198,236 bytes (194 KB)
    MEASURED FREE AT THE ARENA'S OWN DOOR   ~171 KB

The room's own textures cost NOTHING — they stream on entry into a scratch that
is freed — and it registers nothing with texmgr. That was deliberate: the first
pass's six registrations would have been ~110 KB and would not have booted.

THE BOSS IS ROOM-SCOPED, WHICH IS WHAT MAKES ANY OF THIS FIT. The Rabisu used to
hold its model and its one clip from startup and never release them:

    RBSIDLE.PVA    75,776 bytes    ONE idle clip, 19 frames x 496 verts
    RABISU.SMD     28,672 bytes    the model
                  ---------
                  104,448 bytes    for a boss that exists in exactly one room

Both are now loaded from main.c's STATE_LOADING when the destination is the
Garden Courtyard and freed on every other transition — the same move
src/room_arena.c already made for room meshes. THE HEAP, not BSS, and that is
the whole point: unlike a room, NO boss is loaded almost all of the time, so a
BSS block would cost its full size in every room in the game.

>>> AND THE ORDER OF THOSE CALLS IS LOAD-BEARING. IT COST A CRASH TO FIND. <<<
Wiring the model up naively — one if/else, before the sound bank swap — made the
Garden Courtyard die on entry with the signature in
tools/DIAGNOSING_A_BOOT_CRASH.txt section 2. That door is the TIGHTEST MOMENT IN
THE GAME: it is the only transition that loads SND_BANK_BOSS, whose EMERGE clip
is a 71,680-byte malloc, and the 104,448-byte model was being loaded alongside
it. So main.c SEQUENCES them, and the free and the load are deliberately NOT one
if/else:

    /* before the bank swap */
    if (pending_area != STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_free_model();
    if (pending_area != STATE_ASAG_ARENA)       asags_free_model();
    ...
    sound_bank_select(...);
    /* after it */
    if (pending_area == STATE_GARDEN_COURTYARD) rabisus_load_model();
    if (pending_area == STATE_ASAG_ARENA)       asags_load_model();

Free early so the outgoing model is gone before the incoming bank is read; load
late so the bank's buffer is already freed.

    ASGHSLAM.PVA    24,576
    ASGHLAS.PVA     22,528
    ASGHVOM.PVA     20,480
    ASGHFNT.PVA     20,480
    ASGHIDLE.PVA     6,144
    ASGBODY.SMD      6,144
                   -------

##########################################################################
 PART 6A — >>> heap_budget.py WAS WRONG BY 145 KB, AND IT COST A BOSS <<<
##########################################################################
READ THIS BEFORE BELIEVING ANY PRE-SEPTEMBER FIGURE ANYWHERE IN THE TREE.

Every "371 KB free" and "363 KB free" in the first pass's documents was produced
by a script that computed the heap as everything from _end to 0x801FFFF8 and
subtracted only texmgr and the kept buffers. THE TOP 145 KB OF THAT SPAN IS NOT
HEAP. src/main.c declares

    RenderContext ctx;

as a LOCAL in main(), and a RenderContext is two RenderBuffers — each an
OT_LENGTH(2048)-entry uint32 table plus a BUFFER_LENGTH(65536) packet buffer —
so main()'s stack frame is ~148 KB and it sits at the top of RAM for the entire
run. The heap can only ever grow to just underneath it.

WHAT IT COST. The first Asag was sized against the phantom figure: 229 KB of
meshes and clips at half rate, which "fit" 361 KB with 110 KB to spare. The
console crashed on EVERY entry to the arena, by the drop and by the debug menu
alike. Headless, with the model reads instrumented:

    ASGHLAS.PVA -> buffer 0x801D46D0 .. 0x801DE6D0
    sp = 0x801DBD40                      <- the stack pointer is INSIDE it
    Attempted unaligned JR to 0xfe51ffbd from 0x8008D9A0
    -> CdReadSync, cdread.c:160

That is precisely the mechanism tools/HEAP_BUDGET.txt and section 2 of
tools/DIAGNOSING_A_BOOT_CRASH.txt describe: malloc does not fail when the heap
fills, it hands back memory the stack is using and the next CdRead DMAs through
a return address.

>>> AND IT EXPLAINS THE "~234 KB CLIFF" THAT NEVER MADE SENSE. <<< That figure
was measured in the old units. 234 KB reported was about 86 KB real, which is
why a heap with "226 KB free" would not boot. The cliff was never mysterious;
the ruler was wrong.

WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT:
  - tools/heap_budget.py now subtracts a MEASURED stack floor (STACK_FLOOR, read
    as $sp inside read_file on this very transition) and prints the reserve as
    its own line. Re-measure it the same way if main()'s frame ever changes.
  - src/asag.c's read_file() reads $sp and REFUSES any allocation that would
    reach within 8 KB of it. A refused read leaves the mesh or a clip absent —
    a bind pose, or nothing drawn — which is visible and harmless. It can no
    longer corrupt the stack.
  - the clips are baked down (step 3 in this pass; the first pass needed 4).
  - the model load moved after the texture stream, so their peaks do not add.

>>> THE STACK RESERVE IS NOT RECOVERABLE BY MOVING ctx TO BSS. <<< It would move
the same 145 KB from the top of RAM to the bottom and shrink the heap by exactly
as much. What it WOULD do is make the accounting self-evident instead of
depending on a measured constant. Worth doing; not a win.

##########################################################################
 PART 7A — COLLISION: THE ROOM IS A TABLE, THE BOSS IS NOT
##########################################################################
tools/gen_asag_arena_collision.py builds src/asag_arena_mesh_collision.c from
the room proxy alone: 12 walls and 3 floor planes against MAX_WALLS_PER_ROOM
128.

>>> THE BOSS WAS IN THAT TABLE FOR ONE PASS AND HAD TO COME OUT. THIS IS THE
MOST USEFUL THING IN THIS SECTION. <<< Its drawn geometry was converted to 26
static walls, on the reasoning that the mesh the player sees should be the mesh
the player cannot walk through. The walls were baked from frame 1 of
Head_Idle_Baked, where the body rests at y[-960,-528] — 528 above the floor and
342 above the player's eye — so collision.c's vertical gate rejected every one
of them and they never fired at all. They were, honestly, placeholders for the
day the art came down.

The old version of this file said so, and said what to do about it: "when the
encounter animates them, either accept the resting silhouette or replace those
walls with a runtime capsule the way rabisus_collide() does. Do NOT try to
re-bake walls per frame — CollisionRoom is a fixed table read by every movement
query in the frame."

THAT IS EXACTLY WHAT HAPPENED. The position track slides the body up to 969
units into the arena, and the slam and the faint bring it to floor level, so the
baked table went wrong in Z and in Y in the same pass. asag_collide() in
src/asag.c replaces it.

HOW THE RUNTIME COLLIDER WORKS, and the one idea in it worth keeping: it takes
an AABB over ONLY THE VERTICES INSIDE THE PLAYER'S VERTICAL BAND, from the same
posed vertices the draw uses, every frame. Selecting on Y FIRST is what makes a
single box enough. A box round the whole body would be 1669 long in Z and would
wall off a third of the arena whenever Asag leaned in, nearly all of it well
over the player's head. Selecting first means the box is EMPTY for most of the
fight — measured, the idle, the laser and the vomit never reach body height at
all — and small when it is not: the slam is solid for 3.9 of its 6.2 seconds and
the faint for 2.8 of its 5.0.

The band is widened by 60 units (ASAG_SOLID_Y_SLACK) because vertex spacing on
this model is 100-200 units against a 179-unit band, so a steeply descending
part could otherwise cross it with no vertex landing inside and let the player
walk through a leg. More slack than that starts fattening the box while the body
is merely passing overhead, which reads as an invisible barrier.

The rule in tools/ADDING_A_ROOM.txt applies in reverse throughout: read the
height off the mesh, and do not assume a boss occupies the ground just because
it is large. Asag spends most of the fight over the player's head.

NON-LOOPING IS WHAT MAKES A CHAIN WORK AT ALL: a looping clip never reports

hand over to the next. The corollary is that the scene's own phases, which run
UNDER a looping idle, are all on frame counts instead.
