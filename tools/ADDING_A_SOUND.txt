PS1 HORROR GAME — ADDING A SOUND EFFECT: THE COMPLETE RUNBOOK
==============================================================
This is the authoritative, step-by-step procedure for adding a sound effect,
and the reference for the SPU-RAM BANK TIMESHARE. It was written immediately
after the Rabisu's five clips were added (August 2026) — the addition that
exhausted SPU RAM and forced the timeshare into existence — and records every
step that was actually performed, in order, with real commands.

Companion docs (read them for the *why*; this file is the *how*):
  src/sound.h                       - the SPU map and the bank rules, in full
  tools/ADDING_A_BOSS_ENCOUNTER.txt - where a cutscene's sound cues belong
  tools/TEXTURING_NOTES.txt         - the VRAM equivalent of this squeeze
  rebuild commands.txt              - authoritative build steps

NOTE ON PYTHON: the `python` on PATH is the Windows Store stub and fails. Use
`py` or the real interpreter explicitly:
    C:\Users\virtu\AppData\Local\Python\bin\python.exe


##########################################################################
 HOW TO KICK THIS OFF (what the user provides)
##########################################################################
  1. "Follow tools/ADDING_A_SOUND.txt"
  2. The clip, dropped in sounds/ as an .mp3 or .wav.
  3. What fires it, in prose ("when the fireball is loosed").

Everything else — sample rate, VAG conversion, SPU address, bank tag, voice
number, disc entry — is DERIVED by the steps below.


##########################################################################
 STEP 0 — THE ONE FACT THAT GOVERNS EVERYTHING
##########################################################################
SPU RAM IS FULL. There are 512 KB of sample RAM, the first 0x1000 belongs to
the CD-DA capture area, and as of the Rabisu the game uses all but ~16 KB of
what is left. A new effect therefore has exactly three homes, and you must
pick one before writing any code:

  A. RESIDENT — permanent SPU RAM. Only for effects that are SMALL (say under
     16 KB, i.e. ~2.5 s at 11025 Hz) or that must be playable everywhere.
     Costs the remaining headroom, so count it first: see STEP 3.

  B. AN EXISTING BANK — SND_BANK_BOSS if it belongs to the Rabisu's reveal,
     SND_BANK_INTRO if it belongs to the opening sequence (title screen only).
     Free as long as the bank still fits the region (STEP 3 checks this).

  C. A NEW BANK — a set of effects used only in one room, swapped in on the
     transition into it. This is the expensive option; see STEP 7.

>>> A BANKED EFFECT IS SILENT EVERYWHERE ITS BANK IS NOT LOADED. <<<
sound_play() on an evicted effect returns without a sound and without a
complaint. Before banking anything, check every room that plays it — the
check that matters is world.c's placement block, not intuition.


##########################################################################
 STEP 1 — CONVERT THE CLIP
##########################################################################
From an mp3:

    cd C:\Users\virtu\Documents\ps1game\sounds
    py mp3_to_wav.py mysound.mp3 --rate 11025
    py wav_to_vag.py mysound.wav --rate 11025

11025 Hz MONO is the house standard and what every clip but swing.vag uses.
22050 doubles the SPU cost for very little on a PS1's output; do not use it
without a reason. Check what you got:

    py -c "import struct;d=open('mysound.vag','rb').read(48);
    print(d[:4], struct.unpack('>I',d[12:16])[0], struct.unpack('>I',d[16:20])[0])"

Expect b'VAGp', then the audio size, then 11025. The SPU cost is the audio
size rounded up to 64 bytes — NOT the file size, which carries a 48-byte
header.

TRAP: shortening a clip is the cheapest possible way to make it fit. A 5 s
one-shot costs 31 KB; the same effect trimmed to 2 s costs 12 KB. Trim in an
editor before assuming a bank is needed.

COMMIT the .wav and the .vag. git_details.txt calls .wav a source file, not
an intermediate.


##########################################################################
 STEP 1B — LEVEL. THERE IS NO VOLUME KNOB AT RUNTIME.
##########################################################################
>>> A CLIP'S LOUDNESS IS BAKED INTO ITS SAMPLE AND NOWHERE ELSE. <<<

sound_play() sets SPU_CH_VOL_L/R to 0x3fff, which is the MAXIMUM in volume
mode — the register's value is doubled internally, so 0x3fff is 100% and
there is no value above it. Nothing sets the common master volume away from
SpuInit's max either. There is therefore no per-effect, per-room or per-
moment volume in this engine, and "make X louder" always means "re-gain the
wav and re-convert". Do not go looking for a mixer; there isn't one.

    py gain_wav.py mysound.wav --gain 1.5    # +50%
    py wav_to_vag.py mysound.wav --rate 11025

gain_wav.py rewrites the wav IN PLACE (git holds the previous level) and
soft-limits rather than clipping: samples under --knee get the full gain,
above it the curve asymptotes to full scale. It prints the RMS delta, which
is the gain the clip ACTUALLY received — ask for 1.5x on something already
slammed to 0 dBFS and you will get 1.3x, because that is all the peaks left
room for. That number is the honest one; no tool does better.

Length and rate are untouched, so the VAG is byte-identical in size and the
STEP 3 budget does not move.

TRAP: a shared clip is shared. HURT, AXEHIT, SWING, DIE, SLAM, the gun and
the footsteps all play in the boss fight AND everywhere else, so re-gaining
one for one fight re-gains it for the whole game. The five clips that are
genuinely boss-only are FIREBALL, BOOM, EXPLODE, EMERGE and DMNSPEAK; those
were the five taken up 50% in August 2026.

TRAP: EXPLODE's length is load-bearing. RBE_T_D_BURN + RBE_T_D_FADE in
rabisu_boss.c is cut to exactly its 322 frames. Re-gaining does not change
that; TRIMMING would, and silently.


##########################################################################
 STEP 1C — DELIBERATE DEGRADATION (the "retro" grain)
##########################################################################
crush_wav.py is gain_wav.py's opposite number: it makes a clip WORSE on
purpose, for the thin overdriven quality of a 90s survival-horror voice
sample. Same in-place contract, same place in the pipeline:

    py mp3_to_wav.py "My Voice.mp3" --out myvoice.wav --rate 8000
    py crush_wav.py myvoice.wav --bits 6 --drive 2.5
    py wav_to_vag.py myvoice.wav --rate 8000

It band-limits (300-3400 Hz by default), drives into a soft clipper, then
quantises — in that order, because quantising first and filtering after
smooths away the very steps that make the grain audible. NINURTA.VAG is the
worked example: 8000 Hz, 6-bit, drive 2.5.

The SAMPLE RATE is not crush_wav's business — set it in mp3_to_wav.py, since
it is also what decides the clip's SPU cost. Note that dropping to 8000 Hz is
itself most of the effect, and it is the only part of it that saves RAM.


##########################################################################
 STEP 2 — PUT IT ON THE DISC
##########################################################################
disc.xml, inside <dir name="SND">. The disc name is 8.3 UPPERCASE and does
NOT have to match the source filename (dmnspeak.vag ships as DMNSPEK.VAG):

    <file name="MYSOUND.VAG" type="data"
          source="C:\Users\virtu\Documents\ps1game\sounds\mysound.vag"/>

>>> NEVER ADD A SOUND TO THE DISC ROOT. <<< The root directory is FULL — 36
free bytes, less than one record — and the PS1 boot ROM only reads its first
sector to find SYSTEM.CNF. See the comment above the root file list. SND is a
subdirectory precisely so sounds cannot cause this. Verify after any change:

    py tools\check_disc_root.py


##########################################################################
 STEP 3 — DO THE SPU ARITHMETIC *BEFORE* WRITING CODE
##########################################################################
This is the step that gets skipped and it is the one that matters. Nothing at
runtime reports an overflow: load_vag_at() silently drops any clip that will
not fit, and that effect is then mute forever with no message.

Run this, with your clip added to the right list:

    cd C:\Users\virtu\Documents\ps1game\sounds
    py -c "
    import os
    def a(f): return (os.path.getsize(f)-48+63)&~63
    res=['swing','hurt','pickup','smash','axehit','unlock','dropen','DIE',
         'gr_shot','gr_reload','step1','step2','slam','fireball','boom','explode',
         'cursor','select','back']
    house=['dogbark','dogdie','zombie_2','ZOMBIEDIE','tntcl_wrth_2','tntcl_die',
           'spdr_wlk','spit','mchne']
    boss=['emerge','dmnspeak','gate']
    intro=['ninurta']
    p=4112
    for f in res: p+=a(f+'.vag')
    h=sum(a(f+'.vag') for f in house); b=sum(a(f+'.vag') for f in boss)
    i=sum(a(f+'.vag') for f in intro)
    print('bank_base   0x%05X'%p, ' region', 524288-p)
    print('house', h, 'OK' if p+h<=524288 else 'OVERFLOW')
    print('boss ', b, 'OK' if p+b<=524288 else 'OVERFLOW')
    print('intro', i, 'OK' if p+i<=524288 else 'OVERFLOW')
    print('spare', 524288-p-max(h,b,i))
    "

The lists must be kept in the SAME ORDER as the enum in sound.h, because
sound_init() allocates in enum order. As of the garden gate:

    bank_base 0x51C10   region 189424
    house 182656   boss 142528   intro 22080
    spare 6768

(GATE is the worked example of a NON-obvious bank choice, and worth reading
before you place anything else. It is a door sound — the sort of thing that
"obviously" belongs with SFX_DOOR in the residents — but at 17.9 KB it does not
fit the 6.6 KB of resident headroom, and the house bank has exactly the same
6.6 KB free, because `spare` is sized by the LARGEST bank and house IS the
largest. The boss bank had 65 KB free, so the gate went there and the second
room it serves, Fountain Square, was put on the boss bank with the courtyard.
That is only legal because world.c seeds Fountain Square empty: putting a room
on the boss bank costs it all nine monster effects.)

(Before them it was bank_base 0x4F5D0, region 199216, spare 16560. The three
blips took 9792 of that — see STEP 6's note on why they are resident, and note
they are 8000 Hz for exactly this reason: at the house 11025 they would have
cost 13568 and left under 3 KB for everything after them.)

Note `spare` is sized by the LARGEST bank, so a small bank is genuinely free —
adding SND_BANK_INTRO did not move it at all.

RESIDENT costs come off `spare` AND push bank_base up, shrinking the region
by the same amount — a resident clip is charged twice over. A banked clip is
charged only against its own bank's total.


##########################################################################
 STEP 4 — DECLARE IT IN sound.h
##########################################################################
Append to the SfxID enum (append — the ids are positional and sfx_files[] is
indexed by them) and bump SFX_COUNT:

    SFX_MYSOUND = 28,
    SFX_COUNT   = 29,

Give it a comment stating its LENGTH and, if it is banked, saying so. Both
facts are load-bearing later and neither is recoverable from the code.


##########################################################################
 STEP 5 — WIRE IT IN sound.c
##########################################################################
Three arrays, all indexed by SfxID, all of which must stay in step:

  1. sfx_files[]  — "\\SND\\MYSOUND.VAG;1"   (or NULL if it is an alias)
  2. sfx_bank[]   — SND_RESIDENT / SND_BANK_HOUSE / SND_BANK_BOSS
  3. sfx_channel() — only if it needs a voice of its own; see STEP 6

An ALIAS is the fourth possibility and costs nothing: the same sample on a
second voice, for when one clip has two callers that must not cut each other.
SFX_RBS_SWING is the worked example — NULL in sfx_files[], SND_RESIDENT in
sfx_bank[], its own line in sfx_channel(), and one copy in sound_init():

    sfx_slots[SFX_RBS_SWING] = sfx_slots[SFX_SWING];


##########################################################################
 STEP 6 — PICK A VOICE
##########################################################################
The SPU has 24 (0..23). The scheme:

    0        unused
    1..8     the one-shot POOL, FIRST_VOICE + (id % 8)
    9        SFX_NINURTA      (the intro's voice line)
    10       SFX_CURSOR       (the menu blips: off the pool because a menu is
    11       SFX_SELECT        where the player fires sounds back to back at
    12       SFX_BACK          speed, and they must not cut each other)
    13..15   free
    16       SFX_ZOMBIE       (looped ambience)
    17       SFX_TNTCL_WRTH   (looped ambience)
    18       SFX_SPDR_WLK     (HARDWARE-looped ambience)
    19       SFX_EMERGE
    20       SFX_DMNSPEAK
    21       SFX_EXPLODE
    22       SFX_BOOM
    23       SFX_RBS_SWING

Leave it in the pool unless one of these is true:

  - it LOOPS. A looped voice never releases, so it must be alone.
  - it is LONG (say over 2 s) and something in the pool can fire over it.
    Work out which ids share its slot: they are the ones with the same
    (id % 8). SFX_MCHNE is the counter-example — 2.8 s and still in the pool,
    because the puzzle that plays it locks out the two ids that share it.
  - two callers of the SAME sample must overlap. Alias it (STEP 5).

TRAP: a long clip in the pool is the classic bug and it presents as "the
sound sometimes cuts off". The player's footsteps (SFX_STEP1/STEP2, ids 16
and 17) land on pool voices 1 and 2, so ANY long effect sharing those is
chopped every time the player takes a step.


##########################################################################
 STEP 7 — ONLY IF YOU NEED A NEW BANK
##########################################################################
Skip unless STEP 0 landed on option C. Adding a bank is four edits:

  1. sound.h — a new SoundBank value. Number it from 1; zero must stay
     SND_RESIDENT so an effect omitted from sfx_bank[] fails safe.
  2. sound.c — tag its effects in sfx_bank[].
  3. main.c, the STATE_LOADING branch — extend the sound_bank_select() call
     so the new area asks for the new bank. Keyed on pending_area, which is
     why every path into a room (door, stair, title load, debug jump) is
     covered by that one line.
  4. Re-run STEP 3 with the new bank in the list. The region is sized by
     whatever is left after the residents, so a new bank is free until it
     is the LARGEST one, at which point it must still fit the region.

Then check the eviction list against the destination room, in world.c's
placement block. Every effect in every OTHER bank goes silent while yours is
loaded. The house bank is the nine monster effects and it is nine because
world.c places nothing in the Garden Courtyard but the Rabisu.

TRAP: a bank swap is a CD READ (~0.5 s for 120 KB). It can only happen at a
transition, behind the door animation's black screen, with CD-DA suspended —
a CdRead issued while the drive is streaming audio HANGS IT. sound_bank_
select() does the suspend/resume itself; never call it from inside a room.

TRAP: anything that must play DURING a fight has to be RESIDENT. There is no
way to swap a bank in mid-room. This is why the Rabisu's fireball, boom and
explode are resident and only its two reveal clips are banked.


##########################################################################
 STEP 8 — FIRE IT
##########################################################################
    #include "sound.h"
    sound_play(SFX_MYSOUND);

Put the call at the moment the EVENT happens, not at the moment its animation
peaks — a wind-up sound belongs at the start of the wind-up, because the
player is timing a parry off it (see rbs_begin_attack in rabisu.c).

If it is long and a cutscene owns it, add a matching sound_stop() to that
cutscene's reset path. rabisu_boss_reset() stops SFX_EMERGE and SFX_DMNSPEAK
for this reason: a new game started mid-sentence would otherwise carry the
speech into the delivery area.


##########################################################################
 STEP 9 — BUILD AND VERIFY
##########################################################################
    cd C:\Users\virtu\Documents\ps1game
    cmake --build build --target horror_game
    mkpsxiso -y disc.xml
    py tools\check_disc_root.py          # 36 free bytes, both records OK

Then in the emulator, confirm in this order:

  1. The sound plays at all — if it is silent, it did not fit (STEP 3) or the
     disc name does not match sfx_files[] (STEPS 2 and 5).
  2. It is not cut short — if it is, it is sharing a pool voice (STEP 6).
  3. If it is BANKED: walk into the banked room, then walk back out, then
     find a monster of each evicted kind and confirm it still makes its noise.
     A restore that never fires is the failure this whole system invites, and
     it only shows up on the way BACK.
