RAM A+B — TEXTURE STREAMING AND SMD SLIMMING   (started 2026-11-21)
==============================================================
Why: FREE AT REST was 93 KB (tools/HEAP_BUDGET.txt), under heap_budget.py's
128 KB warning line, and the Garden Courtyard door had ~2 KB of slack. The
21 Nov 2026 RAM audit found two safe, frame-rate-neutral reclaims:

  A  texmgr kept every texture the current room uses in main RAM AFTER it had
     been uploaded to VRAM - up to 238 KB (Rear Gate) - although only two
     textures in the whole game are ever re-uploaded mid-room.
  B  every .smd carries a normals array (~28% of the file) that no room, prop or
     boss draw reads, and the 25 resident prop models were read into whole
     2048-byte sectors (79,872 B allocated for 54,548 B of files).

Baseline (before any of this):
    _end            0x80175860
    room arena      124,928 B   (ROOM_ARENA_SECTORS 61, Lamashtu Arena)
    texmgr peak     243,712 B   (Rear Gate's room set)
    kept + chapter   79,872 B   (11 kept + 14 chapter-scoped prop buffers)
    FREE AT REST     95,456 B   (93 KB)

Order: PART A, then PART B. Each part builds, makes a disc and boot-tests on
its own.

Legend:  [ ] to do   [~] in progress   [x] done   [!] problem, see note


##########################################################################
 PART A — TEXMGR STREAMS ITS TEXTURES INSTEAD OF HOLDING THEM
##########################################################################
What changes: a texture's pixels are read from the CD AT ITS UPLOAD (inside
the STATE_LOADING window, where a CD read is legal), DMA'd to VRAM, and freed
straight away. The peak is one TIM instead of the room's whole set.
What does NOT change: registration (header only), the generated per-room
table (tools/gen_tex_rooms.py), every caller of texmgr_upload(), VRAM layout.

The only uploads outside the loading window (call-graph walk from every
texmgr_upload caller, 21 Nov 2026):
    attic_exit.c   attic_exit_unlock_door / relock_door  <- exit_door_puzzle_update
    piano_props.c  piano_props_repair_keys               <- piano_puzzle_update
Those four TIMs are PINNED: held in RAM while a room that wants them is loaded,
which is exactly the old behaviour.
    XTDRLCKD.TIM  XTDRCMPL.TIM  PIANOKEY.TIM  PIANOFUL.TIM

##########################################################################
 PART B — STRIP THE UNUSED NORMALS, SIZE PROP BUFFERS EXACTLY
##########################################################################
What changes: the packed copies on the disc lose their normals array
(n_norms = 0, offsets rebased; the per-prim normal index stays, unread), and
prop models are allocated at file size instead of whole sectors.
What does NOT change: assets/*.smd (smxlink output stays the source), the
per-prim layout (32 bytes; every hard-coded p+16 / p+20 / p+28 still holds),
any draw loop.

WHO READS NORMALS (21 Nov 2026) - and so must NOT be stripped:
    weapon.c weapon_render_model   CRFAXE.SMD (crucifaxe), GRAVOLVR.SMD,
                                   SHARURWP.SMD (via melee_draw_model)
    helluminator.c                 HELLUM.SMD
    crate.c smdSortModel (NCDS)    CRATE.SMD
smdInitData() only rebases the three offsets, so n_norms = 0 is safe.
