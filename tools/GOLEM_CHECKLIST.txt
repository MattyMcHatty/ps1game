GOLEM - ADDING CHECKLIST (Nov 2026)
====================================
Following tools/ADDING_AN_ENEMY.txt. [x] = done, [ ] = to do.

DESIGN (from the brief + answers)
  - Sheet textures/underworld/golem.png, 3x2: 1 2 3 top row, 4 5 6 bottom.
    Idle 1/6, laser 2/3, punch 4/5.
  - 10 HP. Flame Round does half; Helluminator counts as full (not flame).
  - Never moves. Placement may depend on entry door / flags (note in golem.h).
  - Wider than tall, shorter than a Lumberer, Lumberer-sized collision radius.
  - Idle until first damaged; idle frames 1/6 loop at breathing pace.
    Leaving the room before it dies = full health and idle again.
  - Active: PUNCH when in range (Lumberer's attack at half the duration and
    half the shockwave radius, same damage, DARK RED shockwave, SFX_RUMBLE only
    as the arm comes down). Otherwise LASER loop: 1.5 s idle -> 1 s sprite 2
    (glow) -> 2 s sprite 3 beam. Laser always finishes before a punch.
    Aim tracks the player until the beam starts, then is locked; the beam runs
    from the chest centre to the first wall. 25 HP per hit, at most once a
    second. Asag's laser look and sound.
  - Death: SFX_RUMBLE + tablet-style cube burst, brown/red, smaller, tighter
    (spans the golem's width).
  - First placement: Wingscape, in front of the western door.
