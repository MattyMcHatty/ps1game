WINGS TABLET PUZZLE - CHECKLIST (Nov 2026)
==========================================
Wingscape's terrace tablet becomes Chapter 4's second puzzle, on Sharur's
Tablet's pattern (src/sharur_tablet.c). Item work follows
tools/ADDING_AN_ITEM.txt. [x] = done, [ ] = to do.

DESIGN (from the brief + answers)
  - "Press O to interact" sign in front of the tablet's face.
  - Circle: the camera flies to a fixed, face-on shot of the tablet.
  - Then 'HAS WING' fades in at the very centre of the screen, then the
    four answers (BIRD, DOG, CAT, SNAKE) fade in, in a row along the bottom,
    evenly spaced, in a random order that is re-rolled on EVERY room entry.
  - CUNEIFORM ONLY: each word is its line of "wings tablet question and
    answers.png" (top to bottom: has wing, bird, dog, cat, snake). No English.
  - Then the double blue outline reticule (the other puzzles') appears.
    Left/Right move it, Circle answers, Cross leaves at any time.
  - BIRD: the tablet shatters as Sharur's does and drops NINURTA'S WINGS,
    a new collectible. Description: "The wings of a god, carved in stone."
    (sentence case, like the other items).
  - Anything else: the log says "YOU ARE NOT WORTHY", the puzzle closes at
    once, and the player is put on the terrace's cliff edge behind them
    (z~1340, on the tablet's centre line), facing the tablet. A GOLEM
    appears directly in front of the tablet, already awake and attacking.
    Every wrong answer does this.
  - Summoned Golems are PERMANENT, like the placed one (saved; back idle on
    re-entry until killed).
  - GUARDED: while the summoned Golem lives, the tablet ignores Circle and
    shows no sign - so only one is ever summoned at a time, and it can never
    block the puzzle's camera shot.
  - Debug option: puzzle solved, Ninurta's Wings in the inventory, tablet gone.
